# Act I, Movement 1: The Three Doors (Outline)

Companion to Act_I_Outline.md, Excerpt_Bank.md, and Story_Structure.md (Section 12A). Movement name is a proposal (decision).

**Span:** A1 to A3, 15 micro-chapters, about 11,250 words, 2053 to 2069.
**Function:** The gifts arrive, people take them, and each door shows its price.
**Tone:** Wonder, with a cost visible in every chapter.
**Gear mix:** 12 investigative, 3 action, and no hybrids. Each investigative chapter spends about 80% in the Slow Gear, then flips into the Fast Gear for its last three paragraphs. Action chapters open briefly in the Slow Gear and are mostly Fast.

## Movement rules

- The Protagonist has no POV and is never named. He appears only as a card, a note, a hand, or a silence.
- The investing firm, the squeeze, and the cavern do not appear. Nothing here hints at the Reapers or an unnamed enemy.
- No aliens and no Citadel. Warp is about 1 ly/day, so Alpha Centauri is roughly four days out.
- **Emotion is essential (author ruling).** The excerpts' remarks about how the written accounts read, such as A1's "stunned, flat register", describe the in-universe record only and do not flatten the chapters.
- **Chapters run in chronological order** within each excerpt, and the three excerpts follow one another in date order, so the whole movement reads 2053 to 2069 without a backward jump. This departs from the bank's reading order (see Flags).

## A1. Those Who Walked Out Whole (the body) · 3,750 words

**1. Hannah (22), 2053 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: the Galveston clinic at dawn, with antiseptic and Gulf humidity. Her father, who was dying, walks out of the Med-Pod and asks for breakfast. Her deduction is that the flat staff and the missing paperwork mean the clinic is guarding something. She declines the upload and her father does not press her.
- Trigger: a card with no name on it slides under the door.
- Fast close: three or four short lines. She picks it up and the door is empty.
- Plants: her independence, and the nameless card.

**2. OC exposition (mother), 2058 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: a border crossing at night with a dying child, then a queue of families watching a neighbor's cure before trusting it with their own. Her fear is about the cost of trusting.
- Fast close: the neighbor's boy runs past her and the clerk calls a number that is not hers.

**3. OC outlook (official), 2059 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: a district official sent to close a clinic stands in a waiting room among people it has cured. A man who was carried in last week walks past him.
- Fast close: his report to the ministry says only "Nothing found."
- Plants: institutions giving way.

**4. Hannah (28), 2059 [A], 550.**
- Brief Slow Gear: the car park, five months pregnant, a night shift ending.
- Trigger: the bomb. Fast Gear from there, with no thoughts. She moves the night staff out through the corridors and the child is lost.
- Fast close: her husband says no heir while anyone can reach her, and she agrees.
- Plants: the unsolved 2059 bombing for Brooks's file, and the no-heir oath.

**5. Brooks (24), 2062 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: a leukemia ward and a recruiter. She counts exits from habit, walks out cured, and is still counting.
- Fast close: the recruiter asks what she would do with sixty more years.
- Plants: Brooks as the one who counts. The 2059 bombing is the first file she is handed on joining, so the bomb now comes before her arrival.

## A2. The Centuries That Became Days (the stars) · 3,750 words

**1. Brooks (25), 2063 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: Collection work. Three ministries scramble to buy the design, and she tallies their requests.
- Fast close: every form answers the same question with a blank line, "who built it."
- Plants: the blank that A4 builds on.

**2. Hannah (33), 2064 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: the first long-range test crossing. The field hum, the first people past the edge of the Solar System, and four days at a light year a day.
- Trigger: the drive stutters at the midpoint.
- Fast close: instruments and crew all disagree, and no one can say why it should happen.
- Plants: the stutter and her escort role.

**3. OC outlook (old man), 2065 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: the dockside crowd. He dreamed of stars as a boy, watches the first scheduled departure, and weeps without knowing why.
- Fast close: the berth is empty and the crowd is still cheering.
- Note: the wording should say this is an ordinary scheduled passenger crossing, so B4's sailing reads as the expedition.

**4. OC exposition (procurement officer), 2065 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: his engineers find no patent, no inventor, and no lineage.
- Fast close: his one lead is a firm eleven months old.

**5. Hannah (35), 2066 [A], 550.**
- Brief Slow Gear: an escort ship loses its drive mid-crossing.
- Trigger: the field starts to fail. She brings the ship home inside the flotilla's field and runs the drill she wrote for this.
- Fast close: the collapse she is watching is not the one in the drill.
- Plants: the collapse, left unexplained.

## A3. Cheap Hands and Angry Ones (the work of the world) · 3,750 words

**1. OC outlook (laid-off welder), 2067 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: a robot crew raised a district in a season, and he lost his shipyard job that week. His anger is at the job, not the robot.
- Fast close: someone three rows back in the march is carrying a gun.

**2. Hannah (36), 2067 [A], 550.**
- Brief Slow Gear: her firm guards an automation gate.
- Trigger: the picket surges. Fast Gear: she holds the line.
- Fast close: the first stone comes from the roof behind the strikers, not from them.
- Plants: the stone-thrower, and managed unrest.

**3. Brooks (30), 2068 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: Influence work, funding the strikers' legal defense and the politicians who will write the retraining acts. The note is unsigned.
- Fast close: it is in a hand she knows, and it reads "let them be angry, keep them alive."

**4. OC exposition (foreman), 2068 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: disbelief, then orders. He trains the crew that replaces his own.
- Fast close: the machine finishes his drawing before he does.

**5. Hannah (38), 2069 [I], 800.**
- Slow Gear: she hires strikers into her security firm and finds their anger has a use.
- Fast close: one of them asks who owns the robots, and she answers honestly that she does not know all of it.
- Plants: the question A4 sharpens.

## How the movement is built

| Door | Wonder | Price | Closing question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body (A1) | Cures, walking out whole | Violence at the clinic, the offered upload | Who left the card? |
| Stars (A2) | Four days to another sun | Stutter, collapse, a blank where the builder should be | Who built it? |
| Work (A3) | Cities in a season | Anger, and managed unrest | Who owns the robots? |

The Brooks chapters carry the spine of the movement. Each one shows an institution asking the same question and finding it blank, and the handoff into A4 follows from that. Hannah's chapters carry the physical stakes, and the OC voices carry the world's reaction.

## Flags

- **Reordered from the bank.** Excerpt_Bank.md and Act_I_Outline.md keep A1 as 2053, 2058, 2062, 2059, 2059 and A2 as 2064, 2065, 2063, 2065, 2066. This file moves Brooks's 2062 chapter to the end of A1 and Brooks's 2063 chapter to the start of A2. Those two files would need the same change if this order is adopted.
- **Hooks that shift.** A1 now closes on the recruiter's question (sixty more years) instead of the no-heir oath, and A2 now opens on the "who built it" blank before the test crossing. The oath lands mid-excerpt and no longer carries the A1 cliffhanger, so check that A1.5 is strong enough to close the excerpt.
- **A2 opening.** Brooks's 2063 chapter has three ministries trying to buy the design a year before the 2064 test crossing. That is fine if the buyers are reacting to demonstrations or rumors, so the chapter should not imply a public crossing yet.
- **Same-year ties.** The two 2059 chapters (official, then bomb) and the two 2065 chapters (old man, then procurement officer) could swap. The order above puts the institutional beat before the violent one in A1 and the public beat before the paperwork beat in A2.
- **Hannah and the oath.** Her refusal of the upload in A1 sets up her later dissent. Keep her disagreement private, as the act's constraints require.
- **A2.3.** The "first departure" and B4's sailing need distinct wording.
