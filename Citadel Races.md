# Citadel Races: Research Notes

Compiled from the Mass Effect wiki pages and codex summaries listed under Sources. The race sections are canon facts, paraphrased. The Story understandings section is settled fact for this story (author ruling).

---

## Asari

- **Homeworld:** Thessia.
- **Biology:** A single-gender species. They live over 1,000 years across three life stages: Maiden (restless, curious), Matron (settling and raising children), and Matriarch (leaders and councilors).
- **Reproduction:** An asari melds nervous systems with a partner of any species, and the offspring is an asari carrying only her genes.
- **Culture:** Egalitarian, xenophilic, and consensus-driven. They spread influence through culture and intellect and invite new species into the galactic community.
- **Citadel role:** They discovered the Citadel and proposed the Council. They act as its mediators and centrists.
- **Military:** Small and informal. Volunteer commandos handle peacekeeping.

## Salarians

- **Homeworld:** Sur'Kesh.
- **Biology:** Amphibians with a very fast metabolism, needing about an hour of sleep a day. They live about 40 years and have photographic memories.
- **Imprinting:** They imprint psychologically on the clan matriarch (the Dalatrass) at hatching and tend to defer to those they knew in youth.
- **Society:** About 90 percent of the species is male. The rare females hold political power as Dalatrasses.
- **Politics:** The Salarian Union works like a web of noble families. Loyalty is strongest to the first circle of the clan.
- **Culture:** They prize knowledge and embrace the preemptive strike. They reject tradition as restrictive, though the cloistering of females is never questioned.
- **Intelligence:** The Special Tasks Group (STG) runs covert operations in independent cells, including infiltration, sabotage, and assassination. Its work during the Krogan Rebellions was the template for the Spectres.
- **Economy:** Based on cutting-edge technology.

## Turians

- **Homeworld:** Palaven.
- **Biology:** Avian and predatory, with a metallic plated skin that guards against radiation but does not stop projectiles. Their voices have a flanging effect. Their blood is dark blue. They are dextro-amino based, so most food of other species gives them no nutrition and can trigger a fatal allergic reaction. Their lifespan is similar to a human's.
- **Culture:** Militaristic, disciplined, and tied to public service. They are a hierarchical meritocracy. Citizenship is earned through service. Facial markings matter, and a bare face is distrusted.
- **Sayings:** One holds that you see a turian's back only when he is dead.
- **Citadel role:** They filled the military and peacekeeping gap left by the krogan and supply most of the Citadel Fleet. They took the volus as a client race.
- **History:** In canon they opened fire on humans in 2157 over the Council's ban on activating uncharted relays. That war does not happen in this story (author ruling).

## Volus

- **Homeworld:** Irune, a high-pressure world with an ammonia-based biology and 1.5 times Earth's gravity.
- **Biology:** They must wear pressure suits and breathers around other species. They are never seen outside them.
- **Status:** An associate race with its own embassy, shared with the elcor. A client race of the turians, with their government left independent.
- **Culture:** Tribal and dominated by trade, bartering land and even people for status. They refer to other races by source world rather than species name.
- **Economy:** They wrote the Unified Banking Act and balance the Citadel economy. Many are top financial advisers.
- **Grievance:** They have never been invited to join the Council, which is a sore point.

## Hanar

- **Homeworld:** Kahje, a world with 90 percent ocean cover and permanent cloud.
- **Biology:** Jellyfish-like and water-native. They use contra-gravitic levitation packs around other species.
- **Culture:** A benign theocracy, scrupulously polite, with ancient customs governing conversation. They take offense at improper speech. Each hanar has a Face Name known publicly and a Soul Name kept for close friends. They avoid first-person speech and say "this one."
- **Religion:** They revere the Protheans, whom they believe civilized their ancestors, and defend their religious rights vocally.

## Elcor

- **Homeworld:** Dekuuna, a high-gravity world of open plains.
- **Biology:** Massive four-legged creatures that move slowly, an evolved response to an environment where a fall can be lethal.
- **Psychology:** Deliberate and conservative. They prefer open sky and become restless on long starship journeys.
- **Communication:** Ponderous, monotone speech. Among themselves they convey meaning through scent, small movements, and infrasound. They often state aloud when they are being sarcastic, amused, or angry, to avoid misunderstanding.
- **Status:** Associate race sharing an embassy with the volus.

## Batarians

Canon facts, paraphrased from the sources listed under Sources. They were not in the original six races and were added because the relay map places them (author ruling).

- **Homeworld:** Khar'shan, the second planet of the Harsa System.
- **Biology:** Four-eyed anthropoids. Only they and the yahg are known to have more than one pair of eyes.
- **Culture:** They prize social caste and appearance, and overstepping one's place is frowned upon. Slavery is built into the caste system, though Council law forbids it, and batarians see the Council's stance as discrimination.
- **Reputation:** Shrewd merchants. In lawless regions, a batarian negotiation may take place at gunpoint.
- **Government:** The Batarian Hegemony, an authoritarian state. Khar'shan lies outside Citadel jurisdiction, so its population and military figures cannot be checked, and batarian claims about them are propaganda.
- **History with the Citadel:** Around 1785 a batarian fleet bombarded the salarian colony Mannovai. In 1913 the Hegemony annexed the independent asari colony Esan. In 2115 Citadel forces skirmished with batarians on Enael, after which the batarians withdrew into their own systems and are rarely seen in Citadel space.
- **Council status:** They were once part of Citadel space and kept an embassy. In canon the embassy closed after the Council sided with humanity in the Skyllian Verge colonization dispute (humans began colonizing the Verge in 2171).
- **In this story:** There is no Systems Alliance and no First Contact War (author rulings), so the Verge dispute may not happen as written. (open) How the batarian departure from the Council plays out. The map labels Antaeus, Cacus, Farinata, and Plutus as Batarian/Citadel space and Dis as Batarian space.
- **Behavior rule:** A batarian's thoughts, decisions, and actions follow this profile, as with the other races. (open) Any story-specific traits the author wants to add.

## Canon human history (verified)

Checked against the Mass Effect wiki pages and related sources listed under Sources. Items marked (author) are the author's statements and were not found in the sources.

- **Mars and Charon:** Prothean data found on Mars shows that Charon, thought to be a moon of Pluto, is a relay. The Alliance activates it in 2149, and it leads to Arcturus, about 36 ly from Earth. (In this story the data comes from the Mars cavern, not an intact archive, and the activation comes much earlier. See the rulings below.)
- **Arcturus:** A K1.5 III red giant with three planets (Themis, Eirene, Eunomia) and the asteroid Alcyoneus. Several other relays lie close by, which enabled humanity's rapid expansion. The Alliance began building Arcturus Station in 2151, and it became the Alliance's political and military capital.
- **Expansion:** Humans activated every dormant relay they found. The Council had banned this, though humans did not know.
- **Relay 314 Incident (the First Contact War), 2157:** Turians found humans reactivating Relay 314. One human ship escaped, and the turians attacked Shanxi, the human colony nearest the relay. Shanxi is the only human colony ever occupied by aliens, and a human counteroffensive liberated it. The war lasted about three months and cost 623 human lives, with slightly more turian. The Council intervened and negotiated peace.
- **Aftermath:** The Alliance gained the credibility to form its own Parliament and became humanity's face to the galaxy.
- **Not found in the sources:** Shanxi's distance from Arcturus (author: 2 relay jumps, headcanon, treated as true) and the location of Eden Prime in the Attican Traverse (author).

### What the story changes (author rulings)

- **The cavern replaces the archive.** The Mars archive is not intact. The cavern, with its ruined Prothean artifacts and corpses, replaces it, and humanity learns from the cavern that Charon is a relay. This keeps the AU tag while following the canon route.
- **Order of events.** The Protagonist activates the Charon relay first and sprints to the systems 2 jumps from Arcturus. He reveals the cavern afterward, once he has copied the information that still remains in the ruined artifacts. Humanity then opens the relay and settles systems as far as 1 jump from Arcturus, much earlier than canon. After the war, from 2152, the Imperium moves the relay to Adamant.
- **The Relay Quarantine replaces the Emperor's decree (decision).** The Imperium forbids every use of a relay, by anyone, itself included, and holds its relays behind shells with an Imperial Border Gate. The pre-Imperial closing of the ring beyond 2 relay jumps by the Protagonist's forces stands. Relays work for every power until the Imperium removes them from 2152, after the war.
- **War scope.** The war that creates the Imperium covers the 30 ly around Sol and every relay system settled at the time of the conflict.
- The First Contact War does not happen. There is no Systems Alliance.
- Adamant is one of a series of Fortress Systems, each 2 relay jumps from Arcturus. Shanxi is not singled out.
- The Citadel has no data on relays it never used.
- **Relay routes (author ruling).** All routes are in Story Guide Section 6C. In short: Pax (Adamant, also Shanxi) and Fortuna lead into Citadel space, and Han and Ming lead into Batarian space.
- (inference) The Citadel has used its own relays but never the Imperial ones, so it has no data that they reach Pax or Fortuna. The same holds for the batarians and the Han and Ming relays.

## Story understandings (settled facts)

These came from writing the story. The author has ruled that they are generalized and treated as fact. They carry no scene-specific details.

**Imperium**
- Entertains ideas and requests that do not conflict with Imperial law or regulations, or that a citizen wants to spend time and effort on. This never reopens a standing demand or a claim.
- States its demand before using force, and repeats it directly to any armed force it meets. Once the demand is refused, force follows at once, with no wait for a reply.
- Officers answer questions only as far as Imperial law allows.

**Asari**
- Curious and mediating by nature. They suit processing, questioning, and Council roles.
- They study an unfamiliar party to learn what it wants, so they can speak for others.

**Salarians**
- Observe, memorize, and analyze. They suit watcher and analyst roles.
- They record in full so that a reliable record exists when word can finally travel.

**Turians**
- Do not stand down without a fight, but follow protocol first: raise the alert, seek orders from command, hail, and ready weapons. They do not fire first without cause.
- Hail an unidentified armed force to state jurisdiction and demand withdrawal, and to buy time while command cannot be reached. The hail is not a negotiation. The Imperium answers it by repeating its own demand.
- The order to hold overrides compliance with a demand to stand down.

**Volus**
- See disruption through trade and ledgers first, and trace it to its source by following money and goods.
- Ask for a record of what is taken, and the Imperium entertains the request as far as its regulations allow.

**Hanar**
- A refusal must connect to what is actually asked. A hanar declines on allegiance to its own people and faith, politely and formally, and does not object to something the terms never raise.

**Elcor**
- Slow and deliberate, and they say aloud what they feel. They suit the embassy, not field expeditions.
- They advise by stating the time a task will take and how little they expect it to achieve.

**Citadel races in general**
- Every reaction must tie to what has actually been said or done and to what the character could know.
- Every action written in must have a purpose that the character can state.
- The Citadel has no data on relays it never used and cannot know they exist (author ruling). Nothing it could not have seen may be written as something it saw.
- The Citadel does not know that the Protagonist activated Charon first, sprinted to the 2-jump systems, or copied the cavern's artifacts before the reveal. None of it may be written as something a Citadel character saw or knew.
