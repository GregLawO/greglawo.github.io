## 6. Relays and Geography

**Canon rule (author ruling).** Mass Effect canon facts about relays, Arcturus, and Shanxi hold unless the author changes them. The verified canon summary is in Citadel_Races.md, section "Canon human history."

### Sol

- The Sol relay is encased in Charon, Pluto's satellite, and leads to Arcturus, as in canon (author ruling).
- The Protagonist activates Charon first, before humanity knows what it is. He then reveals the cavern. Humanity learns from it that Charon is a relay and opens it (author ruling, see Section 5). Both come much earlier than canon's 2149. (open) The dates.
- The Imperium moves it to Adamant (the Pax System) like every relay in its territory, first of all, in 2152 (see Section 6A and Section 6B). Once it is moved, Sol itself holds no relay, and travel between Sol and Arcturus is by warp (see Section 8A). The relay relinks to its former partners from Adamant (see Section 6B).

### Arcturus

- It lies about 36.7 ly from Earth.
- It is the same as in canon: a major hub with a political seat and military headquarters, with several other relays close by (author ruling, canon).
- It is held by humans and lies inside the Imperium's territory (author ruling). The Systems Alliance is the coalition of the Imperium's enemies, formed in 2135 and defeated in 2150 (author ruling). (open) Who built Arcturus Station, and when.
- It is a contested prize in the interstellar war (author ruling, see Section 4, item 19).
- It is a relay hub until the Imperium removes its relay after the war, beginning in 2152 (see Section 6A). Throughout the war the relays work for every power, and Arcturus is fought over as a hub.

### Relay geography (author rulings)

- Relay routes, jump distances, and Shanxi-Theta (the Imperial Austrine Bastion, home of the world Shanxi) are all in Section 6C. Pax is Adamant and is separate from Shanxi (author ruling).
- **The Relay Quarantine** (decision, replacing the Emperor's decree) forbids every use of a relay, by anyone, itself included. The full text is in Section 6A. The decree's "no exploration past 2 relay jumps" clause is dropped, because no relay can be used at all. The pre-Imperial closing of the ring beyond 2 relay jumps by the Protagonist's forces stands (see below).
- **Before the Imperium (author ruling).** The Protagonist activates Charon first and sprints to the systems 2 jumps away from Arcturus, which prevents expansion beyond. He reveals the cavern afterward, once he has copied the information that remains in the ruined artifacts. Humanity then opens the relay and colonizes systems as far as 1 relay jump from Arcturus. These events come much earlier than in canon, because humanity starts expanding much earlier.
- (inference) The discoveries come after the 2065 cavern find, and humanity's relay settlement ties to the start of expansion around 2075. (open) The dates.
- A relay jump can span hundreds to thousands of ly.
- The Fortress Systems sit 2 relay jumps from Arcturus (see Section 6B).
- The First Contact War of 2157 does not happen.
- The Citadel has no data on any relay it never used, and does not know such relays exist. It never saw the Imperial relays appear, work, or vanish. It also never saw the activation of Charon, the sprint, or the cavern's copying.
- No other species lives in the systems of canon Alliance space, so none lives in Imperial systems.
- (open) How, and when, the Citadel first learns of the Imperium. This is not being worked on.

## 6A. Imperial Territory and the Relay-Moving Ship

### The claim and the Quarantine

- **The claim (author ruling).** After the Imperium is proclaimed, it claims every system between Sol and the Fortress Systems and will enforce its claims by force if necessary. Every system within 100 ly of Sol is occupied (author ruling), and none inside that sphere is left unoccupied.
- **The Relay Quarantine (decision, replacing the Emperor's decree).** Relays are alien artifacts of unknown origin. Each can carry objects from one end of the galaxy to the other, and no one knows what happens if one is damaged or destroyed. The Imperium therefore forbids every use of them, by anyone, itself included, and states the reason publicly. The Protagonist's private reasons are not stated. Before the Imperium, his forces' settlement of the 2-jump systems already closes the ring beyond 2 relay jumps from Arcturus in practice (see Section 6), so no decree is needed for that. Relays work for everyone until the Imperium removes them, from 2152 (see "Stations and relays"). (open) The date the ordinance is promulgated.
- **Relay and non-relay expansion (author ruling).** Relay-based expansion is limited to within 2 relay jumps of Arcturus. The Protagonist's forces reach the 2-jump systems **first**, which are the Fortress Systems, before humanity settles anything. Humanity then settles up to 1 jump after the cavern reveal. Non-relay expansion takes up all the space between the Fortress Systems and Sol.
- **Distances.** The Fortress Systems lie far beyond 100 ly of Sol: Pax (Adamant) at 4,644 ly, Fortuna at 4,709, Han at 11,345, Ming at 11,377, and Shanxi-Theta (Austrine Bastion) at 11,400 (Section 6C).
- **Continuous expansion.** The Imperium will not have enough worlds for its people if it stays within 15 ly of Sol, so it expands continuously. Fifteen light years was only the limit of what the general population's technology and defenses could reach (see Section 4, item 14). The Imperium's ships are not bound by it.
- **Occupation (inference).** A system is occupied when it holds an Imperial presence: a station, an automated outpost, or a colony. Robotics and Resource Collection and Assembly build it, so the pace follows build time, not warp speed. Navy warp (Level Three) crosses 100 ly in about 0.8 days, and Inquisition warp (Level Four) in about 0.4 days.
- **Pace (inference).**
  - 2150 to 2160: the systems within 15 ly of Sol, which the survey already cleared. About 38 star systems (see the count below), roughly 4 a year.
  - 2160 to 2175: everything from 15 ly out to 100 ly, with no system left empty by 2175. About 10,960 star systems, roughly 730 a year, or about 2 a day.
  - After 2175: the ring from 100 ly out to the Fortress Systems, continuing into the Reaper war.
- **System count within 100 ly (ballpark, recomputed for the new radii).** Real stellar density near the Sun is about 4 stars per 1,000 cubic ly, and about 1.5 stars per system. A sphere of 100 ly radius holds about 4.2 million cubic ly, so it holds about 17,000 stars in roughly **11,000 star systems** (a plausible range is 10,000 to 12,500). The 15 ly sphere holds about 57 stars in roughly **38 systems**. A 30 ly sphere, the Reapers' search radius (see Section 8), holds about 450 stars in roughly **300 systems**. About 88 percent of the stars are smaller than the Sun, mostly red dwarfs, and many systems are binaries or multiples. Under the occupation rule above, every one of the 11,000 systems holds an Imperial station, automated outpost, or colony by 2175, whether or not it has a habitable world.
- (inference) The pace jumps by a factor of about 190 between the first and second phases (about 4 systems a year, then about 730 a year). Robotics, Resource Collection and Assembly, and the Navy's lift must grow to match, and the build-up years in Section 4 (items 21 to 23) are where that growth happens.
- **Worlds against population (author ruling).** The Imperium may seem to hold too many worlds for its people. This is mainly due to its use of robotics, Dumb AI, and automation, which build, run, and defend the stations, outposts, and colonies without settlers. (inference) About 11,000 systems against a population of 50 to 90 billion (mid-high tens of billions) is an average of about 5 to 8 million people per system if they were spread evenly. In practice most systems hold only automated stations and outposts, and the people gather in the larger colonies and habitats.
- **No permission.** The Imperium does not need anyone's permission to act. It informs and does not ask.
- **Requests and ideas.** The Imperium is willing to entertain ideas, requests, or anything of a similar nature that does not conflict with Imperial law, does not go against regulations, or that an Imperial citizen feels is something they want to spend time and effort on. This does not reopen the claim.
- **Stations and relays.** The Imperium does not let anyone else run a station inside its territory, and it does not keep a relay there. It moves relays out with the relay-moving ship to Adamant, the Pax System (see Section 6B), and from 2152, after the Alliance concedes, it removes the relay in each relay system. It does not remove relays during the war (2125 to 2150), when every relay stays in use by every power. The relays of the other four Fortress Systems are not carried to Adamant. They are moved only to dark space at the outer fringe of their own systems.
- **Former Relay systems (author ruling).** The Imperium still occupies the 15 ly around every former Relay system, as well as everything within 100 ly of Sol. Arcturus and Euler lie inside the 100 ly sphere. Asgard, Utopia, and Vetus lie far outside it, so their 15 ly spheres are separate holdings. (inference) At about 38 star systems per 15 ly sphere (see Section 8), those three spheres hold roughly 100 to 115 systems.

### The relay-moving ship

- The Protagonist has made a special kind of ship.
- It can attach itself to a Relay and move the Relay to dark space near Adamant, the Pax System (see Section 6B). For the native relays of Fortuna, Han, Ming, and Shanxi-Theta it moves them only to dark space at the outer fringe of their own systems.
- It then detaches and jumps back to where it came from.
- It is a civilian-class relay transporter, up to 20,000 m long (see Section 7A). (inference) A relay transporter is a hauler well over 5 km long, so it is a Crown asset and flies the best warp, taken as Level Five (see Section 7). Its name, build tier, gravitic drive, crew, and first use are open (see Section 13). The trip times are in Section 6B.
- **Transit rule.** A relay being carried to Adamant becomes inaccessible to every relay it connects to, from the moment the ship attaches. **Relinking (author ruling).** Once installed, it relinks to its former partners (see Section 6B).
- **Timing (revised).** Removal begins in 2152, after the war, with Charon first, and runs system by system. The relay-moving ships do not travel with the fleet. The Imperium still needs more than one such ship (the number is open), and the order in which it removes the relays is open.

Each sits in a relay system 2 relay jumps from Arcturus (see Section 6C).

- Each is not a relay dead end. Pax and Fortuna lead to Citadel space, Han and Ming lead to Batarian space, and Shanxi-Theta leads to turian space through Satu Arrd in the Aethon Cluster (Section 6C).
- (inference) He fortifies all five systems at that distance, which together cover every outward route.

### The shell and the Gate

- The Imperium places every Relay within its territory in Adamant, the Pax System (author ruling). The Charon relay is moved there like every other. The relays of Imperial territory (Sol, Arcturus, Euler, Asgard, Utopia, Vetus) and the Pax relay are lifted out of their systems and deposited in dark space near Adamant. The native relays of the other four Fortress Systems (Fortuna, Han, Ming, and Shanxi-Theta) are moved only to dark space at the outer fringe of their own systems (Quarantine, decision, replacing "stay in their own systems"). Each group is enclosed in an enormous shell of automated defense platforms and mines, with a small bare area, the Imperial Border Gate, left for access.
- Relays in Imperial hands are left just beyond the edge of their system, in an area surrounded by automated defensive platforms and mines. Each other Fortress System's native relay is moved to the outer fringe of its own system and lies inside that system's own shell (author ruling, Quarantine). Adamant's shell holds its native relay and every moved relay. No relay in Imperial hands sits outside a shell.
- The only way in is the **Imperial Border Gate**, a small opening in the defensive shell.
- Nobody outside the Imperium can count or observe anything inside the shell.
- (inference) Anything arriving through a relay is already inside the shell and can leave only through the Gate, under Imperial control.

### What the map settles

- **Pax is Adamant** (author ruling). Shanxi is a world in the Shanxi-Theta System (author ruling), which is 2 jumps from Arcturus through Vetus, matching the headcanon that Shanxi lies 2 jumps from Arcturus. Shanxi-Theta is the system where canon placed the First Contact War.
- **Five systems sit 2 jumps from Arcturus:** Pax (Adamant), Fortuna, Han, Ming, and Shanxi-Theta (Austrine Bastion). These are the Adamant Systems (Section 6B).
- **Every Adamant System has outward routes.** Pax leads to Anadius and Fortuna leads to Argos Rho, both Citadel space. Han and Ming lead to Hades Gamma, which is Batarian and Batarian/Citadel space. Shanxi-Theta leads to Satu Arrd in the Aethon Cluster, which is turian space. Every alien arrival at an Imperial shell must come through one of these five systems.
- **Pax is not the only route into Citadel space.** Fortuna is the second, and Shanxi-Theta is a third, into turian space.
- (inference) The Citadel has used its own relays (Anadius, Strenuus, and those in Argos Rho) but never the Imperial ones, so it has no data that they reach Pax or Fortuna. The same holds for the batarians and the Han and Ming relays, and for the turians and the Shanxi-Theta relay (the turians have used the Satu Arrd relay's other routes but have no data that it reaches Shanxi-Theta).
