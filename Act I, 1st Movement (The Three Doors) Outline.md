# Act I, Movement 1: The Three Doors (Outline)

Companion to Act_I_Outline.md, Excerpt_Bank.md, Story_Structure.md (Section 12A), and My Writing Style - The Elastic Pace System (Revised).md. Movement name is a proposal (decision).

**Span:** A1 to A3, 15 micro-chapters, about 11,250 words, 2053 to 2069.
**Function:** The gifts arrive, people take them, and each door shows its price.
**Tone:** Wonder, with a cost visible in every chapter.
**Gear mix:** 12 investigative, 3 action, and no hybrids. Each investigative chapter spends about 80% in the Slow Gear, then closes in the Fast Gear register (short paragraphs, short sentences, the body and not named feeling). What the close carries rotates by ending type. Action chapters open briefly in the Slow Gear and are mostly Fast.
**Release unit:** Each excerpt is one weekly batch of five chapters, read in one sitting. Each batch has an anchor chapter, a quieter middle, and its strongest hook last.

This version is aligned to the revised style. The ending types are read from the hooks in Excerpt_Bank.md. The opening details, body details, batch end lines, echoes, and wants are mine and are proposals (decision).

## Movement rules

- The Protagonist has no POV and is never named. He appears only as a card, a note, a hand, or a silence.
- The investing firm, the squeeze, and the cavern do not appear. Nothing here hints at the Reapers or an unnamed enemy.
- No aliens and no Citadel. Warp is about 1 ly/day, so Alpha Centauri is roughly four days out.
- **Emotion is essential (author ruling).** The excerpts' remarks about how the written accounts read, such as A1's "stunned, flat register", describe the in-universe record only and do not flatten the chapters. The Slow Gear carries emotion through what the character notices. The Fast Gear carries it through the body.
- **Chapters run in chronological order** within each excerpt, and the three excerpts follow one another in date order, so the whole movement reads 2053 to 2069 without a backward jump. This departs from the bank's reading order (see Flags).
- **Every chapter opens on a concrete, question-raising detail.** An opening that only sets the scene is a draft to be fixed.
- **In the Fast Gear, named thoughts and feelings are stripped but one body detail stays**: a shaking hand, a held breath, a grip that will not open.
- **Ending key:** P physical event, Q quiet reversal, B held breath, W withheld answer, E emotional beat with no hook. No more than two P in a batch, and the strongest hook goes last.
- **Voice sheet:** Hannah notices what is missing. Brooks tallies and counts. OC voices notice what they can touch and afford. Counting belongs to Brooks alone in each batch.

## A1. Those Who Walked Out Whole (the body) · 3,750 words

**Batch ends on:** being offered a long life with no say in what it is for.
**Echo:** the Prologue's first door, the body.
**Ending mix:** P, Q, Q, E, W. One physical event. The anchor is A1.4, the quiet middle is A1.2 and A1.3, and the hook is A1.5.

**1. Hannah (22), 2053 [I], 800. End: P.**
- Opens on: the bare wall behind the front desk, where a license should hang.
- Slow Gear: the Galveston clinic at dawn, with antiseptic and Gulf humidity. Her father, who was dying, walks out of the Med-Pod and asks for breakfast. Her habit is to notice what is missing: the flat staff, the absent name tags, the missing paperwork. Her deduction is that the clinic is guarding something. She declines the upload and her father does not press her.
- Trigger: a card with no name on it slides under the door.
- Fast close: three or four short lines. She picks it up, but the step is empty. Body detail: she cannot make her fingers let go of the card.
- Plants: her independence, and the nameless card.

**2. OC exposition (mother), 2058 [I], 800. End: Q.**
- Opens on: a paper slip gone soft in her fist, the ink running, its number unread.
- Wants: her child seen before the day's numbers run out. The lore (families waiting to see a neighbor's cure) is what delays her.
- Slow Gear: a border crossing at night with a dying child, then a queue of families watching a neighbor's cure before trusting it with their own. She notices what she can touch and afford: the weight of the child, the price of the crossing, the slip.
- Fast close: the neighbor's boy runs past her and the clerk calls forty-one, but the slip in her fist says fifty-eight. Body detail: her arms tighten around the child.

**3. OC outlook (official), 2059 [I], 800. End: Q.**
- Opens on: a sealed ministry envelope riding on his knee, the closure order inside.
- Slow Gear: a district official sent to close a clinic stands in a waiting room among people it has cured. A man who was carried in last week walks past him. He notices what he can touch: the warm bench, the stamp in his bag.
- Fast close: his report to the ministry says only "Nothing found."
- Plants: institutions giving way.

**4. Hannah (28), 2059 [A], 550. End: E.**
- Opens on: the third lamp in the car park is out again.
- Brief Slow Gear: the car park, five months pregnant, a night shift ending.
- Trigger: the bomb. Fast Gear from there, with named thoughts stripped. She moves the night staff out through the corridors, and the child is lost.
- Fast close: in the corridor her husband says no heir while anyone can reach her, and she agrees. No hook. The body carries it: her hand rests on her stomach and does not move.
- Plants: the unsolved 2059 bombing for Brooks's file, and the no-heir oath.

**5. Brooks (24), 2062 [I], 800. End: W.**
- Opens on: a hospital bracelet with a number on it and no name.
- Slow Gear: a leukemia ward and a recruiter. Brooks counts exits from habit, since counting is hers alone in this batch. She walks out cured, and is still counting.
- Fast close: the recruiter asks what she would do with sixty more years. The chapter cuts before she answers.
- Plants: Brooks as the one who counts. The 2059 bombing is the first file she is handed on joining, so the bomb now comes before her arrival.

## A2. The Centuries That Became Days (the stars) · 3,750 words

**Batch ends on:** wonder turning to the first fear of the road.
**Echo:** the nameless card of A1.1, now a form with a blank line.
**Ending mix:** Q, P, E, Q, P. Two physical events. The anchor is A2.2, the quiet middle is A2.3 and A2.4, and the hook is A2.5.

**1. Brooks (25), 2063 [I], 800. End: Q.**
- Opens on: three ministry forms stacked together, the same line empty on each.
- Slow Gear: Collection work. Three ministries scramble to buy the design, and she tallies their requests.
- Fast close: every form answers the same question with a blank line, "who built it." Body detail: her pen stops on the third form.
- Plants: the blank that A4 builds on.

**2. Hannah (33), 2064 [I], 800. End: P.**
- Opens on: the pre-flight checklist has no line for the drive.
- Slow Gear: the first long-range test crossing. The field hum, the first people past the edge of the Solar System, and four days at a light year a day. She notices what is missing: no hull markings, no maker's plate, no manual.
- Trigger: the drive stutters at the midpoint.
- Fast close: instruments and crew all disagree, and no one can say why it should happen. Body detail: she counts her own breaths and loses count.
- Plants: the stutter and her escort role.

**3. OC outlook (old man), 2065 [I], 800. End: E.**
- Opens on: the departure board's fare, more than his pension.
- Slow Gear: the dockside crowd. He dreamed of stars as a boy, watches the first scheduled departure, and weeps without knowing why.
- Fast close: the berth is empty and the crowd is still cheering. Body detail: his hands are still raised.
- Note: the wording should say this is an ordinary scheduled passenger crossing, so B4's sailing reads as the expedition.

**4. OC exposition (procurement officer), 2065 [I], 800. End: Q.**
- Opens on: a folder of search results, every stamp reading "no record."
- Wants: the one signature that clears the purchase, before the quarter closes. The lore (no patent, no inventor, no lineage) is what blocks it.
- Slow Gear: his engineers find no patent, no inventor, and no lineage. He notices the weight of the empty files, and not a count of them.
- Fast close: his one lead is a firm eleven months old.

**5. Hannah (35), 2066 [A], 550. End: P.**
- Opens on: the laminated drill card in her hand.
- Brief Slow Gear: an escort ship loses its drive mid-crossing.
- Trigger: the field starts to fail. She brings the ship home inside the flotilla's field and runs the drill she wrote for this.
- Fast close: the collapse she is watching is not the one in the drill. Body detail: the card is bent in her fist.
- Plants: the collapse, left unexplained.

## A3. Cheap Hands and Angry Ones (the work of the world) · 3,750 words

**Batch ends on:** an honest "I don't know" from the person who guards the machines.
**Echo:** the blank "who built it" line of A2.1, now a blank where an owner should be.
**Ending mix:** P, P, Q, Q, Q. Two physical events. The anchor is A3.2, the quiet middle is A3.3 and A3.4, and the hook is A3.5.

**1. OC outlook (laid-off welder), 2067 [I], 800. End: P.**
- Opens on: his last pay slip, folded small, with the rent figure circled.
- Slow Gear: a robot crew raised a district in a season, and he lost his shipyard job that week. His anger is at the job, not the robot. He notices what he can touch and afford: the rent, the cold of the picket sign.
- Fast close: someone three rows back in the march is carrying a gun. Body detail: his sign hand goes slack.

**2. Hannah (36), 2067 [A], 550. End: P.**
- Opens on: no police at the gate, only her own firm's line.
- Brief Slow Gear: her firm guards an automation gate. She notices who is missing from the line.
- Trigger: the picket surges. Fast Gear: she holds the line.
- Fast close: the first stone comes from the roof behind the strikers, not from them. Body detail: the shield shudders against her forearm.
- Plants: the stone-thrower, and managed unrest.

**3. Brooks (30), 2068 [I], 800. End: Q.**
- Opens on: a funding requisition with the approval line empty.
- Slow Gear: Influence work, funding the strikers' legal defense and the politicians who will write the retraining acts. She tallies the sums. The note is unsigned.
- Fast close: it is in a hand she knows, and it reads "let them be angry, keep them alive." Body detail: she sets the page down very carefully.

**4. OC exposition (foreman), 2068 [I], 800. End: Q.**
- Opens on: his pencil drawing, half finished, on the site table.
- Wants: to finish the handover before his crew's last paid shift. The lore (the machines that replace his crew) is what makes the handover painful.
- Slow Gear: disbelief, then orders. He trains the crew that replaces his own. He notices what he can touch: the grain of the pencil, the heat from the machine.
- Fast close: the machine finishes his drawing before he does.

**5. Hannah (38), 2069 [I], 800. End: Q (proposed re-cut: W).**
- Opens on: a service plate on a robot arm with the owner line left blank.
- Slow Gear: she hires strikers into her security firm and finds their anger has a use. She notices what is missing: the owner's name on the plate.
- Fast close: one of them asks who owns the robots, and she answers honestly that she does not know all of it. Under the proposed re-cut, the chapter cuts as she starts to answer, which makes the batch's last beat its strongest.
- Plants: the question A4 sharpens.

## How the movement is built

| Door | Wonder | Price | Closing question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body (A1) | Cures, walking out whole | Violence at the clinic, the offered upload | Who left the card? |
| Stars (A2) | Four days to another sun | Stutter, collapse, a blank where the builder should be | Who built it? |
| Work (A3) | Cities in a season | Anger, and managed unrest | Who owns the robots? |

The Brooks chapters carry the spine of the movement. Each one shows an institution asking the same question and finding it blank, and the handoff into A4 follows from that. Hannah's chapters carry the physical stakes, and the OC voices carry the world's reaction.

## Flags

- **Reordered from the bank.** Excerpt_Bank.md and Act_I_Outline.md keep A1 as 2053, 2058, 2062, 2059, 2059 and A2 as 2064, 2065, 2063, 2065, 2066. This file moves Brooks's 2062 chapter to the end of A1 and Brooks's 2063 chapter to the start of A2. Those two files would need the same change if this order is adopted.
- **Hooks that shift.** A1 now closes on the recruiter's question (sixty more years) instead of the no-heir oath, and A2 now opens on the "who built it" blank before the test crossing. The oath lands mid-excerpt and no longer carries the A1 cliffhanger, so check that A1.5 is strong enough to close the excerpt. Under the revised style, A1.4 ends on an emotional beat and not a hook, which suits this.
- **A2 opening.** Brooks's 2063 chapter has three ministries trying to buy the design a year before the 2064 test crossing. That is fine if the buyers are reacting to demonstrations or rumors, so the chapter should not imply a public crossing yet.
- **Same-year ties.** The two 2059 chapters (official, then bomb) and the two 2065 chapters (old man, then procurement officer) could swap. The order above puts the institutional beat before the violent one in A1 and the public beat before the paperwork beat in A2.
- **Hannah and the oath.** Her refusal of the upload in A1 sets up her later dissent. Keep her disagreement private, as the act's constraints require.
- **A2.3.** The "first departure" and B4's sailing need distinct wording.
- **Strongest hook last.** A3's boldest beat (the stone, A3.2) falls before the fifth chapter. The proposed A3.5 re-cut is one remedy. The other is to keep A3.5 as written and accept that A3 closes on character and not on a blow.
- **Action chapters that end quiet.** A1.4 ends on an emotional beat, not an event. That is intended, since the Fast Gear body does the work, but the draft must make the held hand land.
- **Openings and body details.** The opening details and body details above are proposals. They were written to fit the existing hooks and to avoid hinting at anything the movement rules bar.
