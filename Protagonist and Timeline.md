## 1. Premise

A man wakes up in a world very different from the one he knew when he last went to sleep. He is a **transmigrator** who somehow has inexplicable knowledge **and expertise** in fictional, impossible technologies and techniques. His name in this world is **Kenji Himura**, of Japanese descent (author ruling).

- **Setting:** The Mass Effect universe in 2050. He does not know this at first.
- **Type:** An Alternate Universe (AU). Canon events and details can be changed or broken, but the story mostly follows the canon route (author ruling).
- **Knowledge principle:** Full knowledge and expertise. He knows how to build, use, and bootstrap the technologies, including the tools and manufacturing they need. Receiving it cost him nothing.
- **Reaction on waking:** He is surprised, wonders what happened and why, and immediately suspects he has transmigrated. No answer is ever given.
- **Caution:** Better safe than sorry. He takes his time to secretly build up his foundation.
- **Real-world constraint:** Factories, refining, and growing and training soldiers take time.
- **Method:** He delegates to subordinates and forces.

## 3. Kenji's Arc and Motivation

### Background and early goals
- He grew up poor and lonely in his past life. Being wanted still makes him flinch.
- On waking he understands he is "not in Kansas anymore." He knows the golden rule of survival in an unfamiliar world: patience trumps ambition. He retreats from view and spends years anchoring his foundation.
- He wants a normal life with Hannah. Every gift draws a hostile reaction, and the losses push him from a quiet life toward a defended one.

### Realization
- He does not know he is in the Mass Effect universe when he wakes.
- In his past life he read fan-made novels of Mass Effect. He had decided it was one of the fictional universes he would never want to live in, because of the Reapers. In his view, only ridiculous plot armor ever beat them.
- It is confirmed in 2065, when **Victor Manswell** announces his expedition to Alpha Centauri. The organization names he has been meeting fall into place in the same moment. The Systems Alliance and Cerberus knowledge he woke with falls into place too (inference).
- The reader learns this from his POV. No character is told, and none ever learns how he knows.

### Reaction
- He is terrified, feels vindicated for his caution, and falls into an obsessive drive to keep himself and his people alive.

### Justification for his actions
- He despairs at living in a universe threatened by the Reapers and refuses to leave humanity's survival to a "hero."
- He needs to keep his found family safe: the first dozen servants he recruits between 2053 and 2058, and the people who stay beside them. He does not condition them, and their reactions are organic. Hannah's parents, Tom and Ruth Shepard, are among them. The servants are background characters with no POV.
- **What he tells others (author ruling).** He has no alibi for how he knows what he knows, so he tells the inner circle nothing about the Reapers until much later. His stated justification is a hypothetical: what if what killed the aliens on Mars, or later the makers of the alien FTL artifacts, came back and proved hostile? The dead aliens and the artifacts found across human space justify his obsessive build-up and his plan to unite humanity under his rule.

### Character
- He is an **anti-hero**. He idolizes yet criticizes the God-Emperor of WH40K, for saving mankind and for failing it.
- **Secrecy.** He never makes public, or tells any character, that he is a transmigrator or that he woke with knowledge and expertise. Anyone outside his inner circle learns only what has passed his scrutiny.
- His voice and rules for his POV are in Overview.md.

## 4. Backstory Timeline (2050 to 2175)

### Stage 1: Awakening and caution (2050)
1. **2050.** He wakes with inexplicable knowledge and expertise, suspects he has transmigrated, and chooses to build his foundation in secret.

### Stage 2: The foundation (2050 to 2075)

2. **The servants.** He uses his medical knowledge to gather loyal servants. Clinics run as ordinary businesses offer Med-Pod treatment to the public, and a hidden wing treats recruits with the full technology. Recruits are the dying, the crippled, the abandoned, and skilled professionals with terminal illnesses. Each goes through treatment, a quiet offer, a training upload, and a placement.
3. **Loyalty.** Four layers. Gratitude: the cure is real. Dependence: follow-up treatments come only through his network. Conditioning: the upload secretly carries a directive that makes betrayal unthinkable, including under duress, and the Primaris Process adds a second layer. Compartmentalization: each servant knows only what the role requires. The found family is the exception and is not conditioned.
4. **Four grades.** The cadre receive the altered Primaris Process that keeps human size and staff the hidden service. The managers receive upload and conditioning without implants and run the puppet companies. The staff are ordinary employees who never learn of him. The guard are a few servants who receive the full Primaris Process as his personal protection, and seed the Custodians. The cadre's process reaches three quarters of a full Primaris, and the Custodians grow to 100,000 (author ruling). Levels and biotic tiers are in The_Legions_The_Inquisition_and_The_Custodians.md, Section 10D.
5. **The puppet companies.** Many puppet companies fall into sectors: medical, finance and insurance, industrial automation, space logistics and mining, colony development, media and lobbying, and security contracting. Each sits under layered holding companies in many jurisdictions with a servant-manager at the top, and no company knows the whole network. Income comes from the clinics and from licensing the technologies he allows to spread. The colony companies receive separate charters under different parents, and the charters carry quiet seeds of cooperation (shared lanes, trade agreements, mutual defense compacts) so the colonies' later banding together looks organic.
6. **Setbacks.** Other companies and states join the expansion into space. He accepts this, and the hidden service can sabotage them selectively without exposing the network.
7. **The hidden service.** An intelligence and wet work organization protects the network and becomes his hidden arms and hands, manipulating the political climate to pave the way for his rise. Collection gathers intelligence on governments, rivals, and anomalies. Influence funds pro-colonial politicians, shapes space law and charters, places friends in regulators, and weakens Earth's central authority. Action carries out wet work as a last resort, always disguised as an accident and never repeating a pattern. It is the ancestor of the Inquisition. It forms in 2058 to 2062, after the 2059 bomb.
8. **The conspiracy (author ruling).** From 2059 an organization acts against Kenji. The author-side label is "Cerberus." It is a different organization from canon's, with no link to Harper. Its method is shadow control of the world's most advanced technologies, his above all, through cutouts, firms, regulators, and licensors. It uses no technology his network could trace and has no legal entity. It is behind the 2059 bomb, which hit the clinic at the center of his medical base, and the 2065 withdrawals. The child's death was a cost its planners accepted. It survives the events of 2125 and stays undiscovered by his network. Its payoff depends on how the story unfolds.
9. **Colonies and population.** He slowly builds many human colonies that later band together to revoke Earth's authority and form their own interstellar polity. He also raises humanity's population to the mid-high tens of billions by 2175 (author ruling) through Med-Pod fertility treatment, an end to infant mortality, artificial gestation, and life extension. Humanity is only mildly accepting of artificial gestation, with unease but no organized resistance.
10. **2065. The realization and the expedition.** Manswell announces the Alpha Centauri expedition, and Kenji realizes where he is (Section 3).
    - Kenji is already one of its major investors, through a puppet finance firm. Lenders from several economies also subscribe.
    - Within a few months the lenders withdraw in close succession. The withdrawals are coordinated by the conspiracy. No one in Kenji's network ordered them.
    - Kenji does not withdraw. He raises his stake and becomes the largest contributor so that the expedition goes through. He also offers safer ship designs and longevity treatment through the firm. Manswell accepts in exchange for a controlling stake, receives the managers' upload and conditioning, and stays on as the public face of his company.
    - Brooks and the network see the coordinated pattern and its one exception, Kenji. They conclude that someone is working against Manswell, and possibly against Kenji too.
    - The expedition sails (author ruling). He wants more people to dream of the stars. In the late 2060s it founds a permanent settlement at Alpha Centauri, and its success sparks the colonization wave of the 2070s. Its difficulty was the settling (habitats, power, stores, an escort), not the four-day distance.
11. **2065. The Mars cavern.** (author ruling) A subordinate of his, Teodor Ashby, who oversees the network's droid crews, is the first to find the cavern. Brooks is not. Kenji views the footage and seals it under secret guard. He copies all the information that remains in the ruined Prothean artifacts. The archive is not intact, and nothing is removed except information. He reveals the cavern to humanity only after the copying is finished (Section 5).
12. **2065 onward. The Redoubt Worlds.** Once the cavern is secured, he starts secret construction of five blacksite Redoubt Worlds, each 1,000 ly from the nearest relay. His ships use better warp than the levels he lets spread. The Forge (Ferrum Magna) holds heavy industry, shipyards, and Gundarium. The Cradle (Incubar) holds the Primaris Process and Legion recruit training. The Vault (Arcanum Depths) holds archives, data crystals, and backups of the whole technology base. The Lab (Lumenar Lyceum) studies the cavern information and works on countermeasures. The Haven (Sanctum Ultima) is a refuge if everything else fails. They are separate from the Adamant Systems.

### Stage 3: Interstellar expansion (2075 to 2125)

13. **2075 onward. Colonization.** Nation-states, corporations, and his puppet companies launch colonization efforts, uncoordinated, each settling what its own ships can reach. With Primitive warp (1 ly per day) and the defenses they can field, that reach ends at about 15 ly from Sol (author ruling), a technical limit that no agreement sets. Later histories call it the expansion to fifteen light years.
    - **The spark.** The wave follows from the Manswell expedition's success. He wants that excitement and fuels it.
    - **The Charon relay is a second channel (author ruling).** He activates the Charon relay first, before humanity knows it is a relay, and his forces sprint to the systems 2 jumps from Arcturus (Section 6A of the geography file). Those become the Fortress Systems. He then reveals the cavern. Humanity learns from it that Charon is a relay, opens it in the open, and settles relay systems as far as 1 jump from Arcturus, much earlier than canon. His forces already hold the ring beyond, which stops expansion past it.
    - To reach the 2-jump systems his ships pass through Arcturus and the 1-jump systems. They survey and transit but do not settle them.
    - The activation may be visible (author ruling). No power can contest his technological lead, so he does not need to hide it. It can serve as the public anomaly that prompts the cavern reveal. His own role stays hidden, and puppet companies can front it.
    - **Relays after the war (decision).** Every relay stays in use by every power throughout the war. After 2150 the Imperium issues the Relay Quarantine, forbidding every use of a relay, itself included. Relays are lifted out of their systems and held behind shells with an Imperial Border Gate, beginning with Charon in 2152.
14. **Seeds of tension.** His shadow organization plants tension by orchestrating rivalries and intermittent skirmishes, to prepare an interstellar war that gives humanity experience of navigation, economics, logistics, and warfare.
15. **Engineered tensions.** Four steps, covering the relay colonies as well as the colonies within 15 ly of Sol. Arcturus is a prize in every step.
    - 2075 to 2090: legal and trade disputes over colony charters, tariffs, and belt mining rights, and espionage between the megacorporations.
    - 2090 to 2105: proxy skirmishes at young colonies such as Alpha Centauri, Tau Ceti, and Epsilon Eridani, and at young relay colonies, using mercenaries and raiders funded in secret.
    - 2105 to 2120: naval clashes, blockades, and raids on supply lines between the blocs.
    - **2125: the killing of the UNIN delegates.** Four of the delegates are verified members of the conspiracy acting against Kenji, working with channels inside the Russian Federation, and partly responsible for the 2059 bomb and the 2065 withdrawals. Brooks discovers this and reports it. Kenji decides on the operation, and Brooks carries it out, framing the Russian Federation. Ten innocent delegates die as cover (Human_Characters.md, ruling 15). His motive is revenge. The UNIN, the weakest of Earth's great powers, can only declare before the world that it is helpless to win justice for its slain. It does not annul the charters. The great powers take the confession as a license to judge for themselves.
    - **He answers at once (author ruling).** Immediately after the murder, the leader of his bloc steps forward and declares that where killers go unpunished the sword is the only law left, and that humanity must be taught again what justice and the rule of law are for. He takes up the burden of justice on behalf of all humankind. His ships begin to move that same hour. The declaration is true to him and a lie to the world, because it is also his private verdict on the people who killed his child.
    - A relay is a chokepoint, so relay systems are strong targets for blockades and raids (inference).
16. **The survey.** Beginning before the first colonization efforts, his hidden service surveys every system within 15 ly of Sol and removes all signs of the Protheans and the Reapers. The survey of Alpha Centauri finishes before the expedition sails (inference). The Imperium plays no part, since it does not yet exist. (open) Whether the survey also covers the relay systems within 2 relay jumps of Arcturus.

### Stage 4: The war of conquest (2125 to 2150)

17. **The break.** In 2125, right after the UNIN delegates are murdered, he declares that lawlessness has become the only law. The declaration is immediately followed by the proclamation of the Imperium of Sol (author ruling). He takes the title of Emperor and the claim to every system between Sol and the Fortress Systems, with the promise to occupy every system within 100 ly of Sol. The war lasts from 2125 to 2150. His demand is that every polity concede and face justice, or be crushed. There is no amnesty, and only those who were not involved in the war receive aid (author ruling). Some concede, believing in his cause. Many more refuse and are crushed. All ten Legions are established before the war, each a single Chapter at first, and fight from the start (author ruling).
18. **The sides.** The Imperium fights every other polity. At first the others fight for themselves, and alliances form and break as interests shift. They lose slowly, and in 2135 form the **Systems Alliance** (author ruling), which fights until 2150. The powers in 2125 are:
    - **Canon Earth powers:** the UNAS, the Chinese People's Federation, the European Union, the Russian Federation, the Indian Union, and the UNIN, which claims authority over the colony charters but is the weakest and cannot enforce the claim (author ruling).
    - **Original Earth powers:** the African Concord and the Gulf Compact.
    - **Three canon Mass Effect corporations:** Hahne-Kedar (arms), Ariake Technologies (electronics), and Sirta Foundation (biomedical). Hahne-Kedar declares itself a new polity.
    - **His bloc,** made of his puppet companies, the Southern Cone Republics, the Nusantara Commonwealth, and a few smaller firms.
    - His Fortress Systems belong to his bloc and are not a contested theater unless another power reaches them.
    - (open) Which relay systems each power holds in 2125.
19. **The Systems Alliance (2135 to 2150, author ruling).** Formed in 2135 by the Imperium's enemies. It concedes in 2150 after its last bastion, Arcturus Station, falls.
    - **The Imperium at the start of the war:** 100 Strike Cruisers, 10 Grand Cruisers, and 440 Corvettes in the Legions, plus the Navy's 320 corvettes, 80 frigates, 20 light cruisers, and 5 battleships. Each Legion has a single Chapter of 10,000, so 100,000 Legionnaires.
    - **The Alliance at its formation:** 40 battleships, 80 light cruisers, 240 frigates, 960 corvettes, and an army of 100 million. Its ships fly Primitive drives, which the Imperial Navy outpaces (inference).
    - (open) How the Imperium wins against numbers like these. His technological lead, Primaris and Gundarium armor, robots and Dumb AI, and the growth of the Navy over ten years are the likely answers.

### Stage 5: The end of the war (2150)

20. **The Alliance concedes.** The war ends in 2150. Those who concede face justice. The Inquisition and the Legions remove the leadership of any who keep resisting. The Imperium is still fighting resistance movements on some planets in 2175 (author ruling).

### Stage 6: Rebuilding (2150 to 2175)

21. **2150 to 2160. Integration.** Each polity, whether it conceded or was crushed, is dissolved into a province under Imperial law, and the Inquisition polices it. Veterans from every side are screened. Rebuilding aid goes only to those who were not involved in the war. Resource Collection and Assembly technology rebuilds the aided regions, including relay systems, mostly by 2160. Med-Pod clinics open in every province. From 2152, under the Relay Quarantine, the Imperium removes relays with its relay-moving ships.
22. **2160 to 2170. Build-up.** Industry, fleets, and the Legions grow. The ten Legions fill toward 100,000 each from their home and chapter worlds, and the Imperial Guard garrisons the colonized worlds and installations. The Redoubt Worlds join the Imperium's economy. The population climbs toward its target.
23. **2170 to 2175. Readiness.** The Imperium completes its first line of defenses and drills against a future enemy, as any military does. The Emperor and his inner circle know the enemy is the Reapers. The forces are not told.
24. **2175.** The population reaches the mid-high tens of billions. All ten Legions are complete (author ruling). The Imperium is still fighting resistance movements on some planets.
25. **After 2175.** The build-up continues after the Reapers arrive and the war with them begins.

## 5. Mars

### What is found
- In 2065 the network's droid crews find a vast cavern filled with artifacts, damaged cryo pods, and murdered Protheans. Many artifacts are ruined.
- Video confirms about 1,000 Prothean corpses and significant evidence of a fight, with armored corpses inside and outside the entrance and throughout the cavern.
- **The cavern carries the Prothean information (author ruling).** The archive is not intact. The cavern, with its damaged artifacts, plays the role the Mars data plays in canon, which is to show humanity that Charon is a relay.

### What Kenji concludes
- He assumes the Protheans may have fought a civil war or something similar, and is unsure why they fought and died.
- The Protheans' presence in his own system tells him he is in even more danger.
- Because he knows Mass Effect and realizes in 2065 that he is in it, he already knows Charon is a relay. The copied data confirms it (inference).

### What he does next
1. He seals the cavern under secret guard.
2. He copies all the information that remains in the ruined artifacts.
3. He activates the Charon relay first and sprints to the systems 2 jumps from Arcturus.
4. He reveals the cavern to humanity after the copying is finished. Humanity learns from it that Charon is a relay, opens it, and settles up to 1 jump from Arcturus, much earlier than canon (2149).
- **Nothing is removed from the cavern except information** (author ruling). The ruined artifacts, cryo pods, and corpses stay in place, so humans see the cavern as it was.
- (open) What the surviving information says about the Protheans, the Reapers, and the relays. How he reveals the cavern (a staged discovery, a leak, or an announcement). Where the copied data goes (the Vault, the Lab, or both). The date of each step.
