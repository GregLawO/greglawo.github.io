# Human Characters of Mass Effect in the Imperium Timeline

Every human character from the Mass Effect games, novels, and comics, excluding Andromeda, with canon background, story birth year, and the path they take in this timeline. Non-human characters and AIs are excluded. Ten recurring original characters (OCs) are added in Section 8A. Every entry is decided. Nothing is left for the author to choose, except that the OCs' names, birth years, and links remain proposals (Section 8A).

**How to read it.**
- *Canon birth* is an approximate recollection of wiki data and marked c. where unsure. *Story birth* is what governs.
- Ages are computed by script from the story birth year, so the table, the role checks, and the entries agree.
- (author ruling) in this file means the author's instruction in the request. (ruling) means a decision made here under the author's "auto approved" and now fact.
- Older characters are moved earlier so they are alive before the Imperium. The Reaper-war generation keeps canon-era birth years, so they are 23 to 31 if the Reapers arrive in the canon year of 2183.
- *Chapter POVs* list where a character carries POV in chapters A1 to G5. Section 12 gathers them.

## 1. Rulings introduced by this file

These supersede earlier text where they conflict.

1. **The Protagonist's age (ruling).** His body is thirty in 2050 (born 2020). He marries Hannah in 2058.
2. **Longevity grades (ruling).** Public Primitive Med-Pod gives a healthy span of about 120 years. Full-grade Med-Pod (the Imperial Family, the cadre, Legion veterans, the found family, and not the resistance, whose treatments are public-grade) holds biological age at about thirty to forty for generations. Chronological ages in the table are real. Hannah's biological age is about thirty-five at Alexandra's birth.
3. **Alexandra's birth (ruling).** In 2059, five months pregnant, Hannah survives a bomb at the Galveston clinic complex, but the child she carries is lost. The attackers are never identified and no one claims it. She and her husband swear there will be no heir while anyone can reach her, because an heir is a hostage and a target. Full-grade Med-Pod keeps her fertile, so they wait until the 2150 surrender removes the last enemy who could reach the family, and try again. She conceives in July 2153, and Alexandra is born on April 11, 2154.
4. **The Alliance's last bastion (ruling).** Arcturus Station. Hackett commands the fleet, General Williams the ground forces. Hackett orders the surrender in 2150 to save four million civilians. The Station falls to the 4th Legion.
5. **The resistance (ruling).** Cerberus does not exist. Harper's black cell is the **Third Hand**, inside the **Charter Front**, the umbrella of the unyielding Alliance factions that fight on after 2150.
6. **Biotics (ruling).** Humans are biotic where they were exposed to element zero before birth, at the canon rate. The Inquisition keeps a registry and trains biotics at **Jump Zero on Themis**, the third Arcturus world, using the Systems Alliance human biotic training. The Imperium can also make a natural biotic of a grown human or upgrade an existing one, within the person's base talent. Biotics carry no implants and no migraines, and a biotic who serves in the Navy or the Guard takes the Primaris level of his rank (ruling 23).
7. **No humans in alien space (ruling).** The Citadel has never met a human. Every canon human who appeared on the Citadel or in Citadel space (Udina's embassy, Bailey, Dr. Michel, Brooks) is placed inside the Imperium.
8. **Posts (ruling).** Hannah is Admiral of the Navy, Anderson the Commandant of the Naval Academy at Hesperion, Chakwas the Surgeon-General of the Navy, Udina the Provincial Administrator of Rubicant Hold, Khalisah a licensed commentator, and Zaeed a Chapter Master of the 1st Legion.
9. **Titles (ruling).** Hannah is the Consort. Alexandra is the Heir, addressed as Heir Shepard. Only the Protagonist is Lord.
10. **The Reaper clock (ruling).** The Reapers arrive in the canon year of 2183 which is why the generation of 2150 to 2160 keeps canon-era ages.
11. **Hannah is not told he is a transmigrator (ruling).** She knows he has foresight about the Reapers and learns the enemy's name in 2170. She does not know where the foresight comes from.
12. **The Grand Marshal of the Inquisition (ruling).** Maya Brooks, born 2038, is the Grand Marshal of the Inquisition, appointed at the proclamation in 2125. Ten High Inquisitors and 1,000 Inquisitors serve under her. She is a founding cadre member and a conditioned, altered-Primaris (3/4) spymaster and a Tier 6 biotic. Only the Protagonist outranks her.
13. **The Alliance's members (ruling).** The Systems Alliance of 2135 is made of the UNAS, the Chinese People's Federation, the Russian Federation, the Indian Union, the African Concord, the Gulf Compact, Ariake Technologies, and Sirta Foundation (which falls in 2141), plus the remnants of Hahne-Kedar (whose core falls in 2133). The European Union concedes in 2127 and UNIN is dissolved in 2125. The Southern Cone Republics and the Nusantara Commonwealth are in the Imperium's bloc.
14. **The Charter Front's council (ruling).** Chair Henry Lawson, field commander Petrovsky, fleet chief Ezno, cell chief Coats, security chief Kai Leng, and intelligence chief Harper (the Third Hand).
15. **The framing of 2125 (ruling).** The killing of the UNIN delegates is framed in every chapter and every in-universe document as the act of a rival superpower, the Russian Federation. No writing may contain concrete evidence that the hidden service or the Inquisition did it. The truth (Brooks carried it out) is author-only. Harper suspects that the Russian Federation was framed. He cannot say by whom, and he can prove nothing. The UNIN does not annul the charters. It decries its helplessness to win justice for its slain, and the Protagonist then takes up the burden of justice for all humankind.
16. **Chapter POVs (ruling).** The 32 excerpt chapters (A1 to G5) are told in single-POV micro-chapters, and most of those POVs are characters in this file. The casts are in Excerpt_Bank.md (with the D to F additions in Chapters_D_to_F_Expansion_and_Recurring_OCs.md) and gathered in Section 12. A character carries POV only from age 8, only while alive, and only for what that character could see or know.
17. **The Protagonist carries no POV in these chapters (ruling).** His POV would expose that he is a transmigrator (Section 3). Before 2125 he appears only as seen by Hannah, Brooks, and Manswell. The colonial league's master who declares and proclaims in 2125 is him, so from D4 on he is the public Emperor, seen by every POV.
18. **Victor Manswell (ruling).** He is the founder named in the bible (Section 4, item 11). He is not a Mass Effect game character. See his entry in Section 5.
19. **The early pool (ruling).** Before 2072 the only characters in this file old enough to carry POV are Hannah, Brooks, and Manswell. In chapters A to C, up to two of five micro-chapters per excerpt are OC slots (outlook or exposition). In D to F, at most three of the five new slots (the 33 micro-chapters added in Chapters_D_to_F_Expansion_and_Recurring_OCs.md) may be OC. G keeps one.
20. **Reveal discipline (ruling).** POV chapters may show what a character sees. The lender squeeze and the true nature of the investing firm still never appear in an in-universe document, and the killing of the UNIN delegates appears in no chapter or document except as the act of the Russian Federation (ruling 15).
21. **Democracy against empire (ruling).** The Protagonist chooses empire over democracy and meets resistance from some of his inner circle, chiefly Hannah, who never accepted conditioning, and Maya Brooks, whose conditioning removes betrayal but not disagreement. Members of the found family also divide over it. His justification is that democracy has been given its chance and its endurance has been severely lacking beside history's longest-running empire, whose legacies are far better. He names Rome. The argument is seen only through their POVs (ruling 17).
22. **The Front's technology (ruling).** The Front's technology is public-grade. The Inquisition's members are conditioned, altered-Primaris, and Adjudicator-armored. The Front never takes children from Inquisition custody and strikes only ordinary Guard, provincial offices, and broadcasts.
23. **Primaris levels and biotics (author ruling).** Full Primaris: the Imperial Family and the Custodians. Three quarters: Inquisitors and Legion officers and NCOs, fully conditioned. Half: Legionnaires, fully conditioned. One quarter: Guard elites, officers, and NCOs and Navy officers and NCOs, partially conditioned. Partial conditioning means the subject cannot act against the Emperor or the Imperial Family, carries no secrecy directive, and is free to speak and disagree. Brooks and Bailey are Tier 6 biotics, High Inquisitors Tier 5, Inquisitors Tier 4, and Guard and Navy elites, officers, and NCOs Tier 2 to 4. Full tables are in The_Legions_The_Inquisition_and_The_Custodians.md, Section 10D. Open: the Imperial Family's biotic tiers, and when Brooks and Bailey were made biotics.
24. **The Imperial Family's escorts (author ruling).** The only escorts allowed to the Imperial Family are the Custodians. No Navy, Guard, or other officer holds a protective post over the Family. Friends and staff officers near the Heir are colleagues and companions, never escorts.
25. **Recurring original characters (decision).** Ten original characters recur through Chapters D to F (Section 8A). They follow the grade and conditioning rules of their posts: Reyes and Kessler (Legion NCOs) and Voss (Inquisitor) are fully conditioned, Teixeira (Navy NCO) is partially conditioned at 1/4, and Hale (Guard rank and file) is unconditioned. Their names, birth years, and links are proposals. The F4 Voss slot is an author ruling (auto approved), and the F3 and F4 timing around it is set by the author: the relay ordinance takes effect before the first relay is lifted in 2152, and Voss checks one transporter's manifest at Adamant's Border Gate in late 2152.

## 4. The Imperial Family and the found family

### Hannah Shepard

- **Canon:** Captain in the Alliance Navy and Commander Shepard's mother. She appears in Mass Effect 3 as a stern, capable officer who raised Shepard as a spacer child. Practical, unsentimental about duty, openly warm only toward her daughter.
- **Canon birth:** not stated (implied c. 2130). **Story birth:** 2031. **Side:** Imperium.
- **Chapter POVs:** 29 micro-chapters: A1 (2053, 2059); A2 (2064, 2066); A3 (2067, 2069); A4 (2066); B1 (2065); B2 (2065); B3 (2065); B4 (2068); C1 (2066, 2067); C2 (2066); C3 (2070); C4 (2071); C5 (2072); C6 (2072); C7 (2075); D1 (2078); D3 (2113); E1 (2125); E3 (2128); E4 (2149); F2 (2125); F3 (2153); F4 (2152); G1 (2154); G4 (2170).
- **What changes:** Med-Pod longevity, the escort puppet companies, the war of 2125 to 2150, and the absence of the First Contact War (she is a veteran of the Imperium's war, not the turian one).

Born 2031 in Galveston, Texas, the daughter of a shipyard foreman and a school administrator. Canon names neither parent, and neither is named here. She enlists in the U.S. Navy at 2049 (age 18) and is commissioned an ensign in 2053 (age 22). That same year her father is dying of pancreatic cancer and becomes one of the Protagonist's first recruits, so the Shepards are the first family he grows close to. Hannah refuses the knowledge upload and the conditioning layer. The Protagonist does not press her, and her refusal is the first thing he respects about her. She resigns her commission in 2056 (age 25) to run clinic security and marries him in 2058 (age 27). Full-grade Med-Pod treatment from the wedding on holds her biological age near thirty. In 2059 (age 28), five months pregnant, she is at the Galveston clinic complex when a bomb goes off in its car park. She gets the night staff out and survives, but the child she carries does not. The attackers are never identified, and no one claims it. She and her husband swear there will be no heir while anyone can reach her, because an heir is a hostage and a target, and because he will not found anything on a child's safety. Med-Pod holds her fertile, so the wait costs her only time.

In 2065 (age 34) she commands the escort flotilla that guards Manswell's expedition to Alpha Centauri. Through the 2070s to 2120s she builds the escort and security puppet companies into the hull and crew of the future Navy, and fights convoy actions in the 2105 to 2120 clashes under the public name Captain H. Shepard of the escort contractors, with no visible tie to the Protagonist. At the proclamation in 2125 (age 94) she steps forward as the Emperor's consort and Admiral of the Navy. She argued against an empire the hour before, because she has served navies that answered to elected governments and believes in the vote. He answered that democracy has had its chance and its endurance has been severely lacking beside Rome's, whose legacies are far better. She did not agree, and she stayed, judging the alternative worse. Her war record is Tau Ceti in 2128, Euler in 2133, and the Arcturus siege of 2149.

After the surrender in 2150 she testifies for Hackett at his tribunal. With no enemy left who can reach the family, she and the Emperor try again. She conceives in 2153 and bears Alexandra in 2154 (age 123).

After 2150 she is Admiral of the Navy, the Heir's first teacher, and the Emperor's sharpest critic in private. She is told the enemy is the Reapers in 2170 (age 139). By 2175 (age 144) she commands the whole Navy and has added a non-human adversary to its contingency list, one scenario among many. She receives the Primaris Process at full strength without the conditioning layer (date open) and keeps human size.

**After 2175:** Will hold the Navy through the Reaper war and will not let her daughter be used as a symbol without a ship under her feet.

### Alexandra Shepard (Commander Shepard)

- **Canon:** The Commander. Backgrounds: Earthborn, spacer, or colonist (Mindoir). Careers: Akuze sole survivor, the Blitz, Torfan. Dual temperament: compassionate and ruthless in turn. Unites the galaxy against the Reapers.
- **Canon birth:** 2154. **Story birth:** 2154. **Side:** Imperium.
- **Chapter POVs:** 1 micro-chapter: G4 (2175).
- **What changes:** Born to the Emperor, so every canon background and decision is reframed through Imperial rule, no Alliance career, no alien contact, and the Reapers as a secret her family carries.

Born 2154 (April 11) at the Palace, to the Emperor and Hannah. The circumstances are in Hannah's entry: the lost child of 2059, the oath not to bear an heir while anyone could reach her, and the conception after the 2150 surrender. She is the Heir, addressed as Heir Shepard and never as Lady or Lord.

Childhood is spent between the Palace, Hannah's flagship, and the Naval Academy at Hesperion. Her teachers are Hannah, Admiral Anderson (her godfather), and a Custodian detail that trains her in close combat. Her canon spacer and Earthborn backgrounds both hold: she is born on Earth and raised aboard ships. Her canon colonist background and the Mindoir raid are void, because the batarians never raid human space. In 2170 (age 16) a resistance bombing hits the Guard-held agri-world Thessaly during a state visit. She leads about forty civilians to shelter and holds a door with a Guardsman's rifle until the Legion detachment arrives. That event replaces Akuze and Torfan as the formative one: the canon pair of sole survivor and ruthless commander is split into one night of grief and one night of choosing who to leave behind.

The Emperor knows the canon story and dreads it. He refused to leave humanity's survival to a hero, and his daughter is the person the story would pick. He keeps her off the front line, she pushes against it, and the compromise is a staff post on Hannah's flagship. She enters the Academy at 2172 (age 18), commissions as a lieutenant in 2175 (age 21), and is told on her twenty-first birthday what the Imperium is preparing for. She receives the Primaris Process at full strength that year, unconditioned, and keeps human size. She takes it in silence, asks one question (how long), and receives an honest answer.

Companions of her youth: Kaidan Alenko (a friend since the Jump Zero visit of 2166), Miranda Lawson (a younger classmate and rival), Joker (a pilot cadet whose wit is a disciplinary problem), and Anderson.

**Brooks against Harper: the standing position.**

*What they know of each other.* They have been aware of each other for sixty-three years without meeting. Each knew the other by tradecraft alone until 2125, when Brooks put a name to him. Since 2135 she has held his dossier, face included. Harper knows that the Grand Marshal of the Inquisition is Maya Brooks, because the post is public. He does not know she is the hand behind the accidents, or behind 2125. He has guessed that his escape from Arcturus was allowed, and has not guessed why.

*Limits.* Brooks is conditioned. She cannot harm the Emperor or the Imperial Family, cannot reveal what the Emperor has sanctioned, and cannot betray him, including under duress. She can lie, kill, and arrest freely under Imperial law. Harper is free but short of resources, ageing, and cut off from Imperial technology. He cannot turn her. His only approach is to get her to do something that helps him without her recognizing it as betrayal.

*What each holds.*
- **Brooks:** Udina, Jack as the one witness who has seen Harper in person since 2150, Kasumi and Keiji's partly broken cipher, Miranda's insight into the Lawsons, fourteen captured cells, his dossier, the cover of a file that proves nothing, and the whole of Imperial surveillance.
- **Harper:** public-grade Med-Pod rebuilt from the Sirta archives, Kai Leng, Ezno, Core, Hock's ledger, Lawson's money, Wong's broadcast, and his file on 2125.

*Live gambits.*
- Brooks will have Udina carry a forged cipher that sends the Front to a false supply cache. She will let word of Jack's testimony reach the Front, so that Harper abandons the old Pragia route and shows her where he moves next.
- Harper will cross-check Udina's reports against what his own cells see, to find which are false. He will have Wong broadcast his file on the anniversary of the killing. He will refuse Paul Grayson's plea to retrieve Gillian, because Jump Zero is beyond the Front's reach.

*The secret between them.* Brooks planned the 2125 killings. Harper's file is right that the Russian Federation was framed, and it is unprovable. He cannot say who did it and does not know it was her. The first time she hears the accusation on a pirate broadcast, her conditioning will let her lie but will not let her feel nothing. The Wong broadcast is the first time she hears the accusation aloud.

*The stakes.* If the file is believed in the provinces, the Imperium's founding claim, that it is the law answering a murder, rests on a murder it committed. Harper cannot prove it, but proof is not what a broadcast needs. Brooks cannot answer the accusation without confirming that she fears it.
