# Act I Outline: The Gift

Companion to Story_Structure.md (Section 12A), Excerpt_Bank.md, and the Elastic Pace System (Revised). Act I covers Chapters A to C, 2050 to 2075, in 120 micro-chapters as currently cast. **Revised 1 November 2026 (author ruling):** Kenji's POV now starts in A2 and not A4, A2 grows to 20 micro-chapters and A3 to 10, A4 shrinks to 10, and the new A2 and A3 chapters show the technology he introduces by 2060, robot site security, and (from 2061) the primitive yards and warp tests. Only A1 (5 micro-chapters) is published. A2 and A3 were drafted at 5 each but go live on 2 November 2026, so they are open. B1 to B4 are cast at 10 each, released as two batches of 5. C1 to C7 keep their original casts of 5. C1 to C5 and C7 are extended to 10 each when drafted, and C6 stays at 5. Movement names, endings, batch lines, echoes, wants, and re-cuts are proposals (decision).

## 1. Shape of the act

- **What changes.** Humanity is handed cures and the knowledge to deliver them, then ships and machines, by no one it can name. The reader sees who is behind the gift (Kenji), the network he builds so that no one can find him, the pressure that taught him to build it, and what it costs him. The cavern then shows that humanity was not first, and Charon turns out to be a door.
- **Ends on.** The road runs out at the second jump, and in the letters dread overtakes fascination.
- **The engine.** Each chapter answers one question and raises the next: who is giving us this and what does it cost them (A, B), what lies behind the dead, and will it return (C).
- **Tone.** Wonder in A, strain in B, unease in C. The act closes on a line the in-universe record supplies: after the relay, the question "belonged to locksmiths."
- **Link to the Prologue.** A1, A2, and A3 are the Prologue's three doors (the body, the stars, the work of the world). A2 now carries the answer from inside before the world asks the question in A4.
- **Dramatic irony.** From A2 the reader sees what the in-universe history cannot. Each excerpt is the scholar's account, and the micro-chapters complete or contradict it.

## 2. Chapter by chapter

**Key.** Type: [I] investigative, [A] action, [H] hybrid. Ending (End): P physical event, Q quiet reversal, B held breath, W withheld answer, E emotional beat. Batch line: what a batch of five ends on emotionally. Echo: the earlier element a batch returns to. Wants: the small want and deadline given to each exposition narrator.

*A2a: the build (a fixed past)*

Hook: the lights die, but the machine's panel stays lit, and a man she never saw come in is standing in the dark beside it. (One of Kenji's first operatives, seen only from outside.)
4. A2.4 Kenji (32), 2052 [I] · W. **The network, the first robots, the first operatives.** Shells in several countries and dying professionals hired as managers. A few discarded specialists (a retired analyst, a fixer, a locksmith) who owe him their lives join as operatives. He builds a Level 2 robot and a Level 1 Dumb AI controller in a rented shed, and the robot builds the second Med-Pod. Hook: the first manager asks who the firm answers to, and signs instead. *Tech: Robotics, Dumb AI.*
5. A2.5 Kenji (33), 2053 [I] · E. The Galveston clinic opens. He writes the card with no name and slides it under the door (the other end of A1.1). Tom Shepard walks out whole. Hook: Tom grips his hand and says "son," and he flinches.

## 5. Constraints

- **Kenji's POV (author ruling, revised 1 Nov 2026).** His POV starts in A2, not A4, and carries 7 of A2's 20 micro-chapters, none of A3's 10, and 1 of A4's 10, and at most 3 or 4 in every other Movement 2 chapter. **No Kenji POV is dated after 2064 until B1.2**, so the realization lands there. Before 2065 he has no Reaper thoughts and no thought that this was once a fiction he read, and the knowledge arrives in his hands before his head. He may suspect he has transmigrated. From 2065 the reader sees the Reapers. His own chapters never name shaking hands, and the body carries the feeling through squaring objects and washing his hands. No character is told how he knows what he knows.
- **The conspiracy (author ruling).** It begins with the release of his tech, so the early pressure is its first moves. No POV before 2065 links the incidents, and none names it. Brooks logs the earlier incidents as unrelated files. No in-universe document connects them. The four delegates of 2125 are partly responsible for the 2059 bomb and the 2065 withdrawals only. The earlier probes stay unattributed, so the conspiracy remains larger than those four.
- **The network (author ruling).** The secret network exists before Kenji meets the Shepards, and its operatives are recruited with it in A2.4, before Galveston opens in A2.5. They carry no POV (author ruling). They scout Brooks and make her pitch.
- **Documents.** No in-universe document names the conspiracy, the true cause of the 2065 withdrawals, or Kenji's role in the flare. His name may appear in documents for minor matters.
- **Reapers.** No one outside the inner circle knows or suspects the Reapers. Chapters about "what is on the other side" read as a reaction to the dead, and Kenji's only stated justification to others is the hypothetical about whoever killed the dead.
- **Hannah's dissent** is the only dissent seen in this act. Hannah, Brooks, and Manswell see him from outside.
- No human meets an alien, and the Citadel appears nowhere.
- **Dates** open in the bible (the flare, the reveal, the first public crossing) are proposals. The A2 places and the exact dates of A2.1 to A2.15 are proposals.
- **Register (author ruling).** What the excerpts say about how the written accounts read applies to the in-universe record only. The chapters carry full emotion.
- **Style (author ruling).** The Elastic Pace System, revised for serialization, governs every chapter. In the Fast Gear, named thoughts and feelings are stripped but one body detail stays.
