# Season 1: The Gift

## *The Road to the Stars*, Act I

## The shape of the season

- **Act I: The Gift.** 75 micro-chapters, about 57,750 words, covering 2053 to 2075.
- **15 batches of five micro-chapters.** One batch a week, 15 weeks, each read in one sitting.
- **The question behind it all.** Humanity is handed wonders. The act asks who is handing them over, what it costs, and what is waiting on the other side.
- **The tone.** Wonder in the first stretch, strain in the middle, unease at the end.
- **Season 1 is a complete arc.** It ends on a question, and a pause follows. A rough return window for the next season will be announced at the finale.

## Who tells it

Three recurring narrators carry the act, and ordinary people fill the gaps between them.

| Narrator | Role in the early years |
|---|---|
| **Hannah Shepard** | A young naval officer who becomes a security professional, and a keen noticer of what is missing |
| **Maya Brooks** | A foreign-intelligence analyst who counts, tallies, and sorts |
| **Victor Manswell** | An aerospace entrepreneur with a very large idea |
| **Ordinary people** | Patients, officials, workers, settlers, and clerks, there to show you what the world felt like from the street |

## The four movements

## How to read a batch

- Each batch opens with an **epigraph**, a short quotation from a history of the age.
- Micro-chapters are bylined with their **act, movement, batch code, narrator, place, and year**.
- Micro-chapters inside a batch run in date order, so you never jump backward in a single sitting.
- Batches echo each other. If an object or phrase comes back, notice it.
