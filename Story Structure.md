## 12. Story Structure

- The novel is planned to finish at 300,000 words, told in micro-chapters.
- The novel is written chapter by chapter, starting with the Prologue.
- The first phase focuses on the year 2160. The focal POVs of the first phase are mostly alien species reacting to the emergence of the Imperium.
- The background story is told from a historical accounting perspective or revealed through interaction between the aliens and the humans. The background now includes the cavern reveal and the war across the settled relay systems, within the sanctioned-information rule.
- **The Prologue is a brief history (author ruling).** It is a short in-universe introduction to the events that play out in the chapters up to 2175, in the manner of the opening history of *The Fellowship of the Ring*. It is impersonal and carries no sign of who wrote it. It is narrated in the manner of a voice-over, in demonstrative language that shows the effects of events on the world and avoids technical description (author ruling). It is the file Prologue_The_Road_to_the_Stars.md.
- **The excerpt chapters are part of the main story (author ruling).** The 32 excerpts of the Prologue Excerpt Bank (A1 to G5) each become a chapter of several single-POV micro-chapters, with POVs from various walks of life showing how the event changed their lives. At the 5-micro-chapter minimum that is 160 micro-chapters, about 120,000 words at 750 words each (range 96,000 to 160,000; the bank's own estimate is about 120,150), so they count as part of the main story and not the Prologue. (open) Whether some excerpts are merged to shorten it, and where these chapters sit against the 2160 first phase. (Settled: each chapter's POV cast and cliffhangers are in Excerpt_Bank.md.)
- **Chapter draft format (author ruling).** Each chapter draft opens with its excerpt from Excerpt_Bank.md, at the very top: the excerpt's title only, the text in italics inside quotation marks, and the attribution "— *The Road to the Stars*", then the chapter's own title and prose. The full rule is in Overview.md, Writing Rules.
- **Excerpt register (author ruling).** What an excerpt says about how the written accounts read (for example, A1's "stunned, flat register") describes the in-universe record only. It does not apply to the chapters, which carry full emotion. See Overview.md, Writing Rules.
- **The D to F expansion.** Chapters_D_to_F_Expansion_and_Recurring_OCs.md adds 33 single-POV micro-chapters to Chapters D to F, which takes the excerpt chapters from 160 to 193 micro-chapters and the total from about 120,150 to about 142,850 words (the addition is about 22,700 words, under its 25,000-word cap). They give ground-level voices from the Legions, Navy, Guard, Alliance, and civilian colonies. Casting rule 19 is relaxed for D to F: at most three of the five new slots may be OC, and G keeps one. The F4 Voss slot is an author ruling (auto approved). The names, birth years, and the other added slots remain proposals (decision) until the author confirms them. The additions are merged into Excerpt_Bank.md, which now carries all 193 micro-chapters.

### 12A. Acts and Arcs of the excerpt chapters (decision)

The 32 excerpts (A1 to G5, 193 micro-chapters, about 142,850 words) are divided into three acts and six arcs. The divisions fall between excerpts, so no chapter is reordered. An act is a large block that ends on a major reversal. An arc follows one governing question through its chapters, and each arc sits wholly inside one act. Word counts are estimates computed from each micro-chapter's type tag (investigative 800, action 550, hybrid 750), which gives about 143,500 words in all, against the bank's stated 142,850. The act and arc names are mine and may be revised. Every governing question below is one a POV character or a reader of the in-universe record could ask, and none implies a hidden purpose behind the Imperium's building (see the Reaper secrecy rule in Overview.md).

## 8. The Reapers

- Their FTL speed is 30 ly per day, the canon value per the author.
- They usually clean up the galaxy after each cycle so the next cycle cannot discover how they operate.
- They know Arcturus is a stepping stone toward many unconnected systems. (open) Whether this still holds after the Imperium moves the Arcturus relays.
- They search unconnected systems for developing species, but only within one day of travel from the relay network, and only after they finish wiping out the galaxy for the cycle.
- **Cerberus technology.** He does not use its Reaper research (author ruling), so it adds no indoctrination risk to his people.

### Derived from the Reaper speed (30 ly/day)

- The Reaper search radius around the relay network is about 30 ly.
- **Systems inside the radius (ballpark, author approved).** At the real stellar density near the Sun (see Section 6A), one 30 ly sphere around a relay system holds about 300 star systems. The 25 relay systems on the author's map (Section 6C) hold roughly **5,000 star systems** within 30 ly of any of them. That is 25 spheres of about 300 each, reduced by heavy overlap where relay systems sit close together (Sol, Arcturus, and Euler; the Horse Head Nebula systems; Han and Ming; Exodus's Utopia and Asgard). The plausible range is 4,000 to 7,500. A relay system elsewhere adds about 300 more, less any overlap. Real density rises toward the galactic core and falls toward the rim, so this figure is only a solar-neighborhood estimate. At 15 ly, the Imperial occupation radius around former Relay systems (see Section 6A), one sphere holds about 38 systems, and the 25 mapped relay systems hold roughly **900** (range 700 to 950), with much lighter overlap (only Arcturus and Euler, and Anadius and Strenuus, overlap). Both figures count all 25 relay systems at their original positions, which is the right count because the relays still belong to the network and the Imperium still occupies the 15 ly around the former Relay systems.
- Mark I warp (31.25 ly/day) is slightly faster than the Reapers, and every higher level is faster still.
- The Redoubt Worlds lie 1,000 ly from the nearest relay, far outside the 30 ly radius.
- **The moved relays still count as part of the network (author ruling).** Moved relays remain in the relay network, and the Imperium still occupies the 15 ly around former Relay systems (Section 6A).
- (inference) With every Imperial relay in Adamant, the radius around the Imperial relays now centers on Adamant, with the other four Fortress Systems' native relays and the alien relays as the rest of the network. Sol and the other former Relay systems no longer hold relays.
- (inference) Because moved relays relink to their former partners (author ruling), the network keeps its links. They open from Adamant now.
- **The Reaper radius stays at 30 ly (author ruling).** It is the Reapers' own objective, and the Imperium has no control over it. The Imperial radii (100 ly around Sol and 15 ly around former Relay systems) are the Imperium's own and do not change it.
- (inference) The Imperium occupies 15 ly around Asgard, Utopia, Vetus, and the Fortress Systems, but the Reapers search 30 ly, so the ring from 15 to 30 ly around them is searched and unoccupied. Arcturus and Euler are the exception, because their whole 30 ly spheres lie inside the 100 ly sphere around Sol.
- (open) How continuous expansion changes the Reapers' search and cleanup (see Section 13).
