# Season 1 Plan: The Gift (Act I, A to C)

A release and writing plan for *The Road to the Stars*, Season 1. Act I alone: 75 micro-chapters, about 57,750 words, 15 weeks at one excerpt a week.

## Season 1 map

| Weeks | Excerpts | Movement | Week's job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | A1, A2, A3 | The Three Doors | Hook. Cure, stars, work, each with its price |
| 4 to 8 | A4, B1, B2, B3, B4 | The Gamble | The "who built it?" mystery, then the Manswell rescue and sailing |
| 9 to 11 | C1, C2, C3 | The Dead | The flare, the cavern, the world's reaction |
| 12 to 15 | C4, C5, C6, C7 | The Road | The door opens, the road runs out, and dread overtakes fascination |

Every week is exactly five chapters, so there are no straddling batches. Each excerpt is its own complete batch, with its epigraph at the top and one real hook at the end.

## Launch gate

Two of 75 chapters are drafted. Launch once A and B are drafted (40 chapters), which gives 8 weeks of cushion. Draft C during weeks 1 to 8 at release pace. For zero risk in Season 1, finish all of Act I first, but that delays launch for little gain.

Keep one rule: never let the buffer fall below 3 batches (15 chapters). If it does, announce a short break rather than missing a week silently.

## The break is the real question

Act II is 70 chapters, about 49,250 words, and chapters D to F1 run 7 to 9 micro-chapters per excerpt. At five chapters a week that is about 14 weeks of drafting, so a short break of 2 to 4 weeks will not cover it. Options:

1. **Draft Act II during Season 1.** If you get a few weeks ahead on Act I, put the surplus into planning and drafting D. Then the break can stay short.
2. **Take a longer break and announce the date.** Return only when Act II has at least 5 to 6 weeks buffered, finishing the rest in flight.
3. **Plan during Season 1, draft during the break.** Outline D to F1 while Act I releases, so the break is pure drafting.

Recommended: combine 1 and 3. Outline Act II as you go, and use any slack to draft D1. Announce Season 2's rough return window at the Act I finale, and a firm date only once the buffer exists.

## Making Season 1 work as a season

- **Frame the finale as an ending.** C7 closes on the question "might they return?" That is a strong season-one hook. Label it "Season 1: The Gift" from week 1 so readers know a pause is coming and do not read it as abandonment.
- **Keep the page alive during the break.** Post in-universe archive items (relay map, name glossary, a cast page for Act II) and a progress note every week or two. Sanctioned material only.
- **Say what's next in one line.** A title and a teaser such as "the colonies, the quarrels, the war" gives patrons a reason to stay. Check how your platform handles pausing or skipping billing, since current features are unverified.

## Access

- Free readers get the Prologue and A1 as the hook.
- Patrons get each batch on release day. Free readers get it a week later.
- The in-universe archive (relay map, Ordinance text, name glossary) is the low-cost perk. Release only sanctioned material, never the author-side truth about 2125 or the Reapers.

## Writing changes for a weekly batch

- **One batch is one sitting.** Readers will read all five chapters together, so put the real hook at the end of the batch and vary the endings inside it.
- **Show the excerpt once per batch,** as the epigraph at the top, with only the title and byline on each chapter.
- **Weeks 1 to 4 decide retention.** A1 to B1 must be your best-polished material, so draft and revise them first.
- **Drip option.** If a single weekly drop feels flat, release the batch's chapters across the week at no extra drafting cost.
