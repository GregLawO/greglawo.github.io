## 10B. Armor and Ship Durability

Imperial ships are built with Gundarium Gamma armor and protected by energy-dissipating shields.

Astartes-type armor is protected by energy-dissipating shields. Its wearer survives the blast wave and radiation of a nuclear detonation, but not the fireball. Robots handle entry into any ground the fireball would reach. Adjudicator-type armor survives the blast wave and radiation only at a greater distance from the fireball than Astartes-type armor needs. All Imperial armor is hardened against electromagnetic pulses.

## 8A. Mass Effect FTL and Communications

Canon facts below were checked against the Mass Effect wiki codex pages.

### Drive (canon)

- An element zero core lowers the ship's effective mass, which raises the effective speed of light inside its field. Ships also need conventional thrusters.
- Eezo and power requirements rise exponentially with mass and with the degree of reduction. A field collapse at FTL speed is catastrophic.
- The drive will not fire if a significant object lies in the path of the planned jump.
- Mass relays are the most powerful mass effect engines in existence.

### Drive charge (canon)

- The core builds static charge in proportion to the mass reduction. Undischarged, it discharges into the hull, fusing bulkheads, destroying electronics, and killing the crew.
- Small ships discharge by landing and grounding to a planet. Large ships discharge into a planetary magnetic field, and a gas giant's field can take under an hour. Deep-space facilities such as the Citadel have dedicated discharge facilities.
- Empty space and systems without suitable discharge sites are barriers to exploration. Fewer than 1 percent of stars are explored.

### Speeds and visibility (canon)

- Canon gives no exact speeds. Reapers are believed to cover nearly 30 ly per day, roughly twice what Citadel starships manage. The Andromeda Initiative's Tempest does 13 ly per day.
- A ship at FTL is visible at great distances, but its signature travels at light speed.

### Communications (canon)

- Comm buoys are clusters of miniature relays paired in corridors of low-mass space. Regional networks link through the relays themselves.
- Where a buoy network does not exist or is destroyed, messages move by courier ships and data drones.
- Quantum entanglement communicators (QEC) are instantaneous and point-to-point, but making the particle pairs is delicate and difficult.

### Story rules derived from canon (inference)

1. Removing a relay silences a system. Its buoy links to other regions die at once, and local communication inside the system continues. (inference) A moved relay relinks at Adamant, which restores its jumps there and leaves the buoy links of the system it left dead.
2. After a relay is removed, word leaves the system only by ship, drone, or QEC (open).
3. (Author ruling) The Citadel has no data on relays it never used, so it never sees the buoys or relay traffic of Imperial systems appear or vanish.
4. For non-Imperial ships, relay-less travel takes months to years, and the ship must also find discharge sites across the gap.
5. Anything arriving through a Fortress System's native relay arrives inside the shell and can leave only through the Imperial Border Gate. On the author's map (Section 6C), aliens can reach Imperial space through the Pax relay (from Anadius), the Fortuna relay (from Argos Rho), the Han and Ming relays (from Hades Gamma), and the Shanxi-Theta relay (from Satu Arrd), so any alien ship arrives inside the shell of one of those five systems. (open) Whether and when any does.
6. Imperial warp is Terran warp, not eezo, so it is not bound by discharge limits unless the author says otherwise (open). The new Systems Alliance technology uses element zero, so whether any Imperial ship uses it is open.
7. The Reapers' 30 ly per day implies roughly 15 ly per day for Citadel ships. Imperial Mark I warp (31.25 ly per day) already exceeds the Reapers, and civilian haulers, which now fly up to Level One, are slightly faster than the Reapers too.
8. (Derived) Sol to Arcturus is 36.7 ly: about 37 days at Primitive warp, about 1.2 days at Mark I, about 0.6 days at Mark II, and about 0.3 days at Mark III. Once the Sol relay is moved, this is the only way between them.
