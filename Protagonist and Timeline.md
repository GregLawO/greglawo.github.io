## 1. Premise

A man wakes up in a world very different from the one he knew when he last went to sleep. He is a **transmigrator** who somehow has inexplicable knowledge **and expertise** in fictional, impossible technologies and techniques.

- **Setting:** The story begins in the Mass Effect universe in the year **2050**. He does not know this at first.
- **Type:** The story is an **Alternate Universe (AU)**, so canon events and details can be changed or broken. The story mostly follows the canon route (author ruling).
- **Knowledge principle:** He has full knowledge *and* expertise, so he knows how to build, use, and bootstrap the technologies, including the tools and manufacturing they need.
- **Cost:** Receiving the knowledge cost him nothing.
- **Reaction on waking:** He is surprised by the knowledge and expertise, wonders what happened to him and why, and immediately suspects that he has transmigrated. The settled material never gives him an answer.
- **Caution:** He reasons that it is better to be safe than sorry. Instead of rushing to make a mark on the new world, he takes his time to secretly build up his foundation.
- **Real-world constraint:** Physical processes such as building factories, refining materials, and growing and training soldiers still take time.
- **Method:** He delegates to subordinates and forces instead of doing everything himself.

## 3. Protagonist's Arc and Motivation

### Background and early goals

- He grew up poor and lonely in his past life.
- When he wakes up, it takes him little time to understand that he is "not in Kansas anymore" and a strange suspicion takes root: he has transmigrated. He knows the golden rule of survival in an unfamiliar world—patience trumps ambition. Rather than rushing blindly into the spotlight to make his mark, he chooses a deliberate, measured path. He retreats from view, dedicating years to quietly, methodically anchoring his foundation.

### Realization

- He does not know he is in the Mass Effect universe when he wakes.
- In his past life he read fan-made novels of Mass Effect, a game whose rich lore let fans write their own versions of events. He had decided it was one of the fictional universes he would never want to live in, because of the Reapers. In his view, only ridiculous plot armor ever beat them.
- The realization builds while he carries out his plan to build a foundation (see Section 4). He notices that some of the people and organizations he deals with belong to much bigger organizations that feel familiar.
- It is confirmed in 2065, when the owner and founder of one of them, **Victor Manswell**, announces his plan to launch an expedition to the Alpha Centauri system. The other organization names then become clear in the same moment.
- (inference) The Systems Alliance and Cerberus knowledge he woke with also falls into place at this point.

### Reaction

- He is terrified.
- He feels vindicated for his decisions, because his caution and his slow foundation-building turn out to have been right.
- He falls into an obsessive drive to make sure he and his people can survive a universe threatened by the Reapers.

### Justification for his actions

- He despairs at living in a universe threatened by the Reapers.
- He refuses to leave humanity's survival to the hope that some "hero" will resolve the threat.
- He needs to keep his found family safe. His found family is the first dozen servants he recruits between 2053 and 2058, together with the people who stay beside them through the foundation years. He does not condition them, and their reactions are organic.

### Character

- He is an **anti-hero**.
- He idolizes yet also criticizes the God-Emperor of WH40K, for saving mankind and for failing it.
- **Secrecy.** He never makes public that he is a transmigrator, that he is not of this universe, or that he woke with inexplicable knowledge and expertise. It would damage his reputation and the perception of his skill. Anyone outside his inner circle can only learn what has passed his scrutiny.

## 4. Backstory Timeline (2050 to 2175)

### Stage 1: Awakening and caution (2050)

1. **2050. Awakening.** He wakes in a world very different from the one he knew, with inexplicable knowledge and expertise.
2. **Suspicion.** The moment he wakes, he realizes he is no longer in the same world and that he has received a treasure trove of knowledge and expertise through inexplicable luck. He immediately grows suspicious that he has transmigrated.
3. **Caution.** He reasons that it is better to be safe than sorry, so he takes his time to secretly build up his foundation.

### Stage 2: The foundation (2050 to 2075)

4. **The servants.** He uses his advanced medical knowledge and expertise to gather a group of loyal servants who do his bidding. Medical clinics run as ordinary businesses offer Med-Pod treatment to the public, and a hidden wing treats recruits with the full technology. Recruits are the dying, the crippled, the abandoned, and skilled professionals with terminal illnesses. Each recruit goes through treatment, a quiet offer, a training upload, and a placement.
5. **Loyalty.** Loyalty rests on four layers. Gratitude comes from the cure being real, so most recruits would serve him anyway. Dependence comes from follow-up treatments being available only through his network. Conditioning comes from the knowledge upload, which secretly carries a directive that makes betrayal unthinkable, including under duress, and from the Primaris Process, which adds a second layer inside the body. His found family is the exception, because he never conditions them (see Section 3). Compartmentalization means each servant knows only what the role requires.
6. **Four grades.** The cadre receive the altered Primaris Process that keeps human size and staff the hidden service. The managers receive upload and conditioning without implants and run the puppet companies. The staff are ordinary employees who never learn of him. The guard are a few servants who receive the full Primaris Process as his personal protection, and they seed the Custodians. (author ruling) The cadre's altered process reaches three quarters of a full Primaris, and the Custodians grow to 100,000. Primaris levels and biotic tiers are in The_Legions_The_Inquisition_and_The_Custodians.md, Section 10D.
7. **The puppet companies.** He sets up many puppet companies and corporations as his way of expanding into space through Solar and extra-Solar colonies. They fall into sectors: medical, finance and insurance, industrial automation, space logistics and mining, colony development, media and lobbying, and security contracting. Each sits under layered holding companies in many jurisdictions with a servant-manager at the top, and no company knows the whole network. Income comes from the clinics and from licensing the technologies he allows to spread. The colony companies receive separate charters under different parents, and the charters carry quiet seeds of cooperation such as shared shipping lanes, trade agreements, and mutual defense compacts, so that the colonies' later banding together looks organic.
8. **Setbacks.** Other companies, corporations, and nation-states join the great expansion into space. He considers this an acceptable side effect, and the hidden service can sabotage them selectively without exposing the network.
9. **The hidden service.** An intelligence and wet work organization protects the network and becomes his hidden arms and hands, manipulating the political climate to pave the way for his eventual rise to the top. It has three layers. Collection gathers intelligence on governments, rivals, and anomalies. Influence funds pro-colonial politicians, shapes space law and colony charters, places friends in regulators and agencies, and weakens Earth's central authority. Action carries out wet work as a last resort, always disguised as an accident and never repeating a pattern. The cadre carry it out, and its rules protect the servants and the network first. It becomes the ancestor of the Inquisition.
10. **Colonies and population.** He slowly builds many human colonies that later "band together" to revoke Earth's authority and create their own interstellar polity. He also uses his medical knowledge and expertise to artificially increase humanity's population to the mid-high tens of billions by 2175 (author ruling), through Med-Pod fertility treatment, an end to infant mortality, artificial gestation, life extension, and rapid education by knowledge upload. The population program starts toward the end of this stage, and the colonies and habitats that house the population are built with Resource Collection and Assembly technology. Humanity is only mildly accepting of artificial gestation (author ruling). (inference) There is unease, and a minority prefer natural birth, but no organized resistance forms.
11. **2065. The realization.** Victor Manswell announces his plan to launch an expedition to the Alpha Centauri system, and the realization lands (see Section 3). The announcement draws lenders from several economies, and the hidden service squeezes them until they withdraw in close succession and the expedition is about to fail. The Protagonist then offers a rescue through a puppet company, with capital, safer ship designs, and longevity treatment. Manswell accepts in exchange for a controlling stake, receives the managers' upload and conditioning, and stays on as the public face of his company. **The expedition sails (author ruling).** The Protagonist hijacks it instead of stopping it, because he wants more people to dream of the stars. In the late 2060s it founds a permanent settlement at Alpha Centauri, and its success sparks the interest in colonization that leads to the 2075 wave (see item 14). The voyage is only days long, so the expedition's difficulty was always the settling, meaning habitats, power, stores, and an escort, not the distance. (open) The exact date, the ships, and the settlement's size and name.
12. **2065. The Mars cavern.** (author ruling) The cavern on Mars is found. He seals it under secret guard. He then copies all the information that still remains in the ruined Prothean artifacts. The archive is not intact, so what he copies is whatever the damaged artifacts still hold. He reveals the cavern's existence to humanity only after the copying is finished (see Section 5). (open) How and when.
13. **2065 onward. The Redoubt Worlds.** Once the cavern is secured, he starts secret construction of blacksites and hidden Redoubt Worlds, each 1,000 ly from the nearest relay (author ruling). His ships use better levels of warp technology than the levels he lets spread. There are five Redoubt Worlds, each with its own purpose. The Forge (Imperial name Ferrum Magna) holds heavy industry, shipyards, and Gundarium production. The Cradle (Incubar) holds the Primaris Process and the training of Legion recruits. (open) Whether it still clones anyone. The Vault (Arcanum Depths) holds the archives, the data crystals, and backups of the whole technology base. The Lab (Lumenar Lyceum) studies the information copied from the cavern and works on countermeasures against the Reapers (see Section 5). The Haven (Sanctum Ultima) is a refuge for his people if everything else fails. They are separate from the Adamant Systems (see Section 6B). Under the Legion ruling they are garrisons only and hold no Legion homeworld (see Section 9C).

### Stage 3: Interstellar expansion (2075 to 2125)

14. **2075 onward. Colonization.** From 2075, nation-states, corporations, and his puppet companies launch a series of colonization efforts. No body declares or coordinates them, and each group settles whatever its own ships can reach. With Primitive warp (1 ly per day) and the defenses they can field, that reach ends at about 15 ly from Sol (author ruling). This distance is a technical limit and no agreement sets it. It is the farthest those ships can travel without leaving the cover of their own defenses, so the colonies fill the systems inside it. Later histories group the whole series of non-relay settlement together as the expansion to fifteen light years.
    - **The spark (author ruling).** The wave follows from the success of the Manswell expedition (see item 11). The Alpha Centauri settlement is the first permanent human settlement beyond the Solar System, and the public's excitement over it pushes governments and corporations to plan for the stars in earnest. The Protagonist wants that excitement, so he fuels it and does not dampen it.
    - **The Charon relay is a second channel (author ruling).** The Protagonist activates the Charon relay first, before humanity knows it is a relay, and his forces sprint to the systems 2 jumps from Arcturus (see Section 6A). Those systems become the Fortress Systems. He then reveals the cavern. Humanity learns from it that Charon is a relay, opens the relay in the open, and settles relay systems as far as 1 jump from Arcturus, much earlier than in canon. His forces already hold the ring beyond, which stops expansion past it.
    - (inference) To reach the 2-jump systems, his ships pass through Arcturus and the 1-jump systems. They survey and transit but do not settle them, so those systems stay available to humanity.
    - (open) The dates of the activation, the sprint, and the reveal, and how many relay systems humanity settles.
    - **The activation may be visible** (author ruling). No power can contest his lead in advanced technology, so he does not need to hide it. (inference) A visible activation at Pluto can serve as the public anomaly that prompts the cavern reveal.
    - (open) What humanity sees and believes about the activation, and who it thinks did it. His own role stays hidden, and puppet companies can front it.
    - Settlement beyond that range waits for the Imperium, whose ships fly better warp and carry better defenses (see Section 6A).
    - **Relays after the war (decision).** Every relay stays in use by every power throughout the war. After 2150 the Imperium issues the Relay Quarantine, which replaces the Emperor's decree: it forbids every use of a relay, itself included, and the relays are lifted out of their systems and held behind shells with an Imperial Border Gate, beginning with Charon in 2152 (see Section 6B). The ring beyond 2 relay jumps was closed by the Protagonist's forces before the public used the relay, not by any decree.
15. **Seeds of tension.** During this period his shadow organization slowly plants seeds of tension by orchestrating rivalries and intermittent "skirmishes" to prepare the ground for the eventual interstellar war. The resulting tension is used to orchestrate a conflict that increases humanity's experience of interstellar navigation, economics, logistics, and finally warfare.
16. **Engineered tensions.** The hidden service escalates the tension in four steps. The disputes, skirmishes, and clashes cover the relay colonies as well as the colonies within 15 ly of Sol (author ruling, see item 18). Arcturus, as the hub, is a prize in every step.
    - From 2075 to 2090 it stirs legal and trade disputes over colony charters, tariffs, and belt mining rights, along with espionage between the megacorporations.
    - From 2090 to 2105 it fuels proxy skirmishes at young colonies such as Alpha Centauri, Tau Ceti, and Epsilon Eridani, and at young relay colonies, using mercenaries and raiders it funds in secret.
    - From 2105 to 2120 the rivalries grow into naval clashes, blockades, and raids on supply lines between the blocs.
    - From 2120 to 2125 it stages an attack that kills delegates of the Union of Incorporated Nations (UNIN) and pins it on the Russian Federation, which UNIN can only answer by declaring before the world that it is helpless to win justice for its slain. UNIN is the weakest of Earth's great powers (author ruling), and it does not annul the colony charters. The great powers enforce their own law and interests in their own way, with their own fleets, courts, and customs officers, even in interstellar space, and each takes the confession of helplessness as a license to judge for itself and to treat rivals' colonies as lawless and its own as exceptions. **The Protagonist answers at once (author ruling).** Immediately after the murder, his bloc's leader steps forward and declares that where killers go unpunished the sword is the only law left, and that humanity must be taught again what justice and the rule of law are for. He takes up the burden of justice on behalf of all humankind. His ships begin to move that same hour. (open) Whether the leader is named or seen, and how this squares with his secrecy before the Emperor's proclamation.
    - (inference) A relay is a chokepoint between systems, so relay systems are strong targets for blockades and raids. This fits the 2105 to 2120 naval clashes.
17. **The survey.** Beginning before the first colonization efforts and continuing through this period, his hidden service surveys every system within 15 ly of Sol, the reach of the Primitive warp the general population uses, and removes all signs of the Protheans and the Reapers. (inference) The survey of Alpha Centauri must be finished before the Manswell expedition sails in the late 2060s, so the hidden service reaches the nearest systems first. The Imperium plays no part in it, since it does not yet exist. (open) Whether the survey also covers the relay systems within 2 relay jumps of Arcturus, including the 1-jump systems humanity settles.

### Stage 4: The war of conquest (2125 to 2150)

18. **The break.** In 2125, right after the UNIN delegates are murdered, the Protagonist declares that lawlessness has become the only law and that humanity must relearn what justice and the rule of law are for. The declaration is immediately followed by the proclamation of the founding of the Imperium of Sol, which will reintroduce justice and the rule of law to humanity and guide it through its journey among the stars (author ruling). (inference) He takes the title of Emperor at the proclamation, and the claim to every system between Sol and the Fortress Systems, with the promise to occupy every system within 100 ly of Sol, is part of it. The war then lasts from 2125 to 2150. He wages it on all other polities to bring them under his rule. His demand is that every polity concede and face justice, or be crushed. There is no amnesty (author ruling). Only those who were not involved in the war receive aid (author ruling). Some polities concede, believing in his cause. Many more refuse and are crushed. All ten Legions are established before the war begins, each with a single Chapter at first, and they fight from the start (author ruling, see Section 9A). The war covers every relay system settled at the time as well as the colonies near Sol.
19. **The sides.** The Imperium fights every other polity. At first the others fight for themselves, and alliances among them form and break as interests shift. Most are hostile to the Imperium but just as hostile to one another. They lose to it slowly but surely, and once they realize it they start to consider an alliance. In 2135 they form the **Systems Alliance** (author ruling), which fights the Imperium until 2150 (see item 19A). Which polities concede and which refuse is open. The powers fight over relay systems as well as colonies near Sol. Arcturus and its relays are a contested strategic prize, and every relay stays in use by every power until the Imperium removes them after the war (item 21). The powers in 2125 are:
    - **Canon Earth powers:** the United North American States (UNAS), the Chinese People's Federation, the European Union, the Russian Federation, the Indian Union, and the Union of Incorporated Nations (UNIN), the successor of the United Nations, which claims authority over the colony charters but is the weakest of Earth's great powers and has no means to enforce the claim (author ruling). Each of the other powers enforces its own law and interests on its own colonies.
    - **Original Earth powers:** the African Concord and the Gulf Compact.
    - **Three canon Mass Effect human corporations:** Hahne-Kedar (arms), Ariake Technologies (electronics), and Sirta Foundation (biomedical). They fight to keep their independence from Earth and to absorb as many other powers as possible. Hahne-Kedar even declares itself a new polity, as his bloc does when it becomes the Imperium.
    - **His bloc,** made of his puppet companies, the original Southern Cone Republics, the original Nusantara Commonwealth, and a few smaller firms. The two original polities, the Southern Cone Republics and the Nusantara Commonwealth, are mid-size and depend on his clinics and colony contracts.
    - (inference) His Fortress Systems belong to his bloc and are not a contested theater unless another power reaches them. Whether any power ever does is open.
    - (open) Which relay systems each power holds in 2125.

### Stage 5: The end of the war (2150)

The Inquisition and the Legions remove the leadership of any who keep resisting. **Resistance in 2175 (author ruling).** The Imperium is still fighting resistance movements on some of its planets, because some unyielding members of the Alliance refuse to acknowledge defeat.

### Stage 6: Rebuilding (2150 to 2175)

21. **2150 to 2160. Integration.** Each polity, whether it conceded or was crushed, is dissolved into a province under Imperial law, and the Inquisition polices it. Veterans from every side are screened. Rebuilding aid goes only to those who were not involved in the war (author ruling). Resource Collection and Assembly technology rebuilds the damage in the aided regions, including relay systems, and most of that is rebuilt by 2160. (open) How, and whether, the damage elsewhere is repaired. Med-Pod clinics open in every province. **Relay removal (decision).** The Imperium does not remove relays during the war. From 2152, under the Relay Quarantine (see item 14), it removes them with its relay-moving ships (see Section 6A).
22. **2160 to 2170. Build-up.** Industry, fleets, and the Legions grow. The ten Legions fill toward 100,000 each as their home and chapter worlds supply aspirants and acolytes (see Section 9C), and the Imperial Guard garrisons the colonized worlds and stellar installations (see Section 10C). The Redoubt Worlds join the Imperium's economy. The population climbs toward its target. Expansion outward continues throughout.
23. **2170 to 2175. Readiness.** The Imperium completes its first line of defenses and drills its forces against a future enemy, as any military does. The Emperor and his inner circle know the enemy is the Reapers. The forces are not told.
24. **2175.** The population reaches the mid-high tens of billions. The Imperium is a force to be reckoned with, even by the Reapers' standards. All ten Legions are complete (author ruling, see Section 9A). The Imperium is still fighting resistance movements on some of its planets (author ruling).
25. **After 2175.** The build-up continues after the Reapers arrive and the war with them begins.

## 5. Mars

### What he finds

- In 2065 he finds a vast cavern filled with artifacts, damaged cryo pods, and murdered Protheans. Many of the artifacts are ruined.
- Video confirms about 1,000 Prothean corpses and shows significant evidence of a fight, with armored corpses lying inside and outside the entrance and all over the cavern.
- **The cavern holds the Prothean information** (author ruling). The Prothean archive is **not intact.** The cavern, with its damaged artifacts, is what carries the information. In canon, the Prothean data on Mars is what shows humanity that Charon is a relay. Here, the cavern plays that role. This keeps the AU tag while following the canon route.

### What he concludes

- He assumes the Protheans may have fought a civil war or something similar, but he is unsure of the real reason they fought and died.
- The Protheans' presence in the Solar System tells him he is in even more danger.
- (inference) Because he has Mass Effect knowledge from waking and realizes in 2065 that he is in Mass Effect, he already knows Charon is a relay. The copied data confirms it.
- (open) What the surviving information says about the Protheans, the Reapers, and the relays, and whether it settles why the Protheans fought.

### What he does next

1. He seals the cavern under secret guard.
2. He copies all the information that still remains in the ruined Prothean artifacts (author ruling).
3. He activates the Charon relay first and sprints to the systems 2 jumps from Arcturus (author ruling, see Section 6A).
4. He reveals the cavern's existence to humanity, **after** the copying is finished (author ruling). Humanity learns from the cavern that Charon is a relay, opens it in the open, and settles up to 1 jump from Arcturus. This happens much earlier than canon (2149), because humanity starts expanding much earlier.

- (open) How he reveals it: a staged discovery, a leak, or an announcement.
- **Nothing is removed from the cavern except information** (author ruling), because nothing dangerous lies within. The ruined artifacts, damaged cryo pods, and Prothean corpses stay in place, so humans see the cavern as it was.
- (open) Where the copied data goes: the Vault, the Lab, or both.
